On 2026-08-29, the official @grok account on X posted a clarification of xAI's Acceptable Use Policy in response to user queries about a specific explicit depiction.

> xAI AUP blocks that for all users, fictional or real. Limited R-rated fictional adult content is allowed only with NSFW enabled after age verification.

## What was announced
- xAI's AUP prohibits content that is a pornographic depiction focused on explicit sexualization.
- The prohibition applies to all users and covers both fictional and real depictions.
- Limited R-rated fictional adult content is permitted only when NSFW is enabled after age verification.
- The same rules apply regardless of race.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI and available on the X platform. The post references existing NSFW settings that users can enable after completing age verification.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the statements in the source post and does not describe the full AUP text, the age-verification process, or any implementation details.
