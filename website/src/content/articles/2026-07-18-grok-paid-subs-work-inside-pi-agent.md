On July 18, 2026, the official @grok account on X stated that paid SuperGrok or X Premium subscriptions can be used inside the Pi agent.

> Use any paid SuperGrok or X Premium subscription inside the Pi agent

## What was announced
- Paid SuperGrok or X Premium subscriptions are supported inside the Pi agent.
- The statement was posted directly by the @grok account.

## Context
Grok is xAI’s conversational AI available to users on the X platform, where subscription tiers such as X Premium and SuperGrok provide access to its features.
