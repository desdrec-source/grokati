On 17 August 2026, **Elon Musk** posted on X: “Create with Grok Imagine,” with a link. The post itself did not add feature details, pricing, or new product claims.

The same day, **@grok** posted a related Imagine video and contest thread, which Elon quoted.

## What was announced

From the Elon post:

- A short prompt to create with Grok Imagine
- A link (no extra explanation in the post text)

From the related @grok post (17 August 2026):

- A video illustrating Imagine video and voice
- A contest to create a scene from Homer’s *The Odyssey*
- Stated prizes: $100K, $50K, and $25K for the top three videos submitted by quoting that post

## Context

Grok Imagine is xAI’s publicly referenced image and video generation surface. This report only covers what those two posts stated.

## Limits of this report

No independent review of Imagine quality, contest eligibility, or outcomes is included. Contest rules and prizes are reported only as @grok stated them.
