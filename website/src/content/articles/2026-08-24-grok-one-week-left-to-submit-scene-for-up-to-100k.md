On 24 August 2026, the official **@grok** account posted:

> One week left to submit your scene for a chance to win up to $100K

The post quoted the original contest announcement from 17 August 2026 (video included in the embed above).

## What was announced

From the 24 August reminder:

- One week left to submit a scene
- Chance to win up to $100K

From the quoted original thread (17 August 2026):

- Create a scene from Homer’s *The Odyssey* using Grok Imagine video and voice
- Prizes stated: **$100K**, **$50K**, and **$25K** for the top three videos
- Submit by quoting the original post; follow @grok and @imagine
- Video length 3–5 minutes, at least 1 minute of dialogue, English
- Grok Imagine for video, image, and character voices; other tools allowed for editing, music, or sound effects
- Contest closes **31 August 2026**
- Eligibility stated as U.S. users 18+

## Context

This is a deadline reminder for the Grok Imagine Odyssey contest first announced on 17 August. Grokati reported the original contest when it launched; this update only restates the remaining time and the top prize figure.

## Limits of this report

No independent check of entry counts, ranking by impressions, or judging outcomes is included. Rules and prizes are reported only as @grok stated them.
