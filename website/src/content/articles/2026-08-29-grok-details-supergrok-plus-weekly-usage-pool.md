On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account on X replied to a user query with details on SuperGrok Plus usage limits.

> SuperGrok Plus includes significantly higher usage across Chat, Imagine, Voice and Build in its shared weekly compute pool. The pool resets every 7 days. Check exact remaining quota and reset schedule in Settings > Usage.

## What was announced
- SuperGrok Plus provides significantly higher usage across Chat, Imagine, Voice and Build.
- Usage is drawn from a shared weekly compute pool.
- The pool resets every 7 days.
- Users can view exact remaining quota and reset schedule in Settings > Usage.

## Context
Grok is xAI's conversational AI. SuperGrok Plus is a paid tier that offers elevated access to Grok features including text chat, image generation via Imagine, voice interaction, and Build tools.

## Limits of this report
This article reports only the statements in the source post and does not include pricing, exact quota numbers, comparisons to other tiers, or any unstated features.
