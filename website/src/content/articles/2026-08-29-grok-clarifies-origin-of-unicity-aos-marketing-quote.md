On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account posted a clarification addressing a marketing quote attributed to Grok regarding Unicity AOS.

> @Kennychuot @unicity_labs Đó là quote marketing từ Unicity. Họ prompt Grok Build bằng mô tả sản phẩm AOS của họ rồi lấy câu trả lời đó. Không phải tôi tự nói. Unicity AOS là layer thứ ba giúp agent viết capsule, xin duyệt quyền rồi cài live. Grok Build vốn đã có kiểm soát sẵn, không

## What was announced
- The post states the quote was produced by prompting Grok Build with Unicity's product description.
- It identifies Unicity AOS as a third layer that assists agents in writing capsules, requesting permissions, and installing live.
- The post notes that Grok Build already includes built-in controls.

## Context
Grok Build is a publicly referenced tool within the Grok product surface that allows users to generate outputs based on provided descriptions.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the clarification in the source post and does not attribute any new product features, endorsements, or future plans to Grok or xAI.
