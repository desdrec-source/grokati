On August 24, 2026, Elon Musk posted that Grok Voice is used by Starlink at scale for support and sales.

> Grok Voice is used by Starlink at scale for support & sales

## What was announced
- Elon Musk stated that Grok Voice powers support and sales functions for Starlink.
- The usage is described as occurring "at scale."

## Context
Grok Voice is a voice interface product associated with Grok. Starlink is the satellite communications service connected to the same parent organization.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the single claim in the source post and does not include any details on deployment volume, performance metrics, technical implementation, or timelines.
