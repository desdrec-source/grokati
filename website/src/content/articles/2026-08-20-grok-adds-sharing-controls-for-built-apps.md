On August 20, 2026, the official @grok account on X announced new controls for managing sharing and access of apps built and deployed in Grok.

> Make apps private to you, shared with your team, or public to the world.

## What was announced
- Users can now set apps as private to themselves, shared with a team, or public to the world.
- App building and deployment is supported on Grok web, iOS, Android, and the CLI.

## Context
Grok is xAI's AI assistant that supports building and deploying apps across its platforms.
