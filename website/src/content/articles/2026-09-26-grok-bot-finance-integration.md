On 26 September 2026, the official **@bot** account announced a Finance integration for Grok Bot.

> Grok Bot now connects to your finances.
>
> Link your bank, card, and investment accounts with the new Finance integration, then ask Bot to help manage your spending, investments, and more.

In a follow-up in the same thread, @bot wrote that accounts connect through Plaid, access is read-only, Grok Bot never sees logins, and accounts can be unlinked at any time. It directed users to search for “Finance” in the marketplace and linked https://x.ai/bot.

Elon Musk quoted the announcement the same day: “Grok @Bot can manage your finances.”

## What was announced

From the **@bot** posts only:

- A Finance integration that can link bank, card, and investment accounts
- Connections run through Plaid
- Access is described as read-only
- Grok Bot does not see account logins
- Users can unlink at any time
- The integration is found by searching “Finance” in the marketplace

## Context

Grokati has previously recorded other official Grok Bot connectors and marketplace items. This brief covers only the 26 September Finance announcement and Elon’s quote. An older June 2026 x.ai news post about Interactive Brokers inside Grok is a separate product and is not treated as the same launch.

## Limits of this report

The source is two @bot posts plus Elon’s quote. The posts do not name supported banks or brokerages, plan gating, regions, write actions, trading, bill pay, or what “help manage” includes beyond read-only account access. No x.ai news page for this integration was published at the time of this brief.
