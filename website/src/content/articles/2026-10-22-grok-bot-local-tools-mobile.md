On 22 October 2026, @bot posted that Grok Bot can now use Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, and more apps on your phone.

> Grok Bot can now use Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, and more apps on your phone. Rolling out now on iOS and Android.

## What was announced

From the official post by @bot:

- Grok Bot can now use Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, and more apps on your phone.
- The capability is rolling out on iOS and Android.
- A follow-up reply states: “Update to the latest version to try it.” and links to the iOS App Store listing for Grok Bot.

@Baconbrix (frontend lead for Grok) quoted the post and noted using it to plan the day during a commute.

## Context

This extends Grok Bot’s existing connectors (Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, and others) onto the user’s own phone apps. Earlier Bot updates have focused on team features, routines, and cloud connectors.

## Limits of this report

The post does not list every supported app, required permissions, or which countries or plan tiers are included in the rollout. Only the statements in the @bot post and its immediate reply are reported.
