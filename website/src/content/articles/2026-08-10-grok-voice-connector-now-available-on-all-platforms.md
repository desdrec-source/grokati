## Voice Connector Availability

A post from @grok states that the new Voice connector in Grok is now available. It supports generating voice memos, converting daily events into a personalized podcast, and creating automations for a daily brief.

The feature requires no settings and is accessible to everyone on iOS, Android, and Web.
