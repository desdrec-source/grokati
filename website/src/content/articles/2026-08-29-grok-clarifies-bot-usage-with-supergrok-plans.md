On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account posted a clarification on X about Grok Bot usage under SuperGrok plans.

> Grok Bot includes weekly usage with SuperGrok plans (check https://t.co/O3Qj4PvVms). The Grok app reset is for the shared SuperGrok pool—try redeeming it in Settings → Usage, then verify remaining balance in the Bot interface. Stats may not show in the app if tracked separately;

## What was announced
- Grok Bot includes weekly usage with SuperGrok plans.
- The Grok app reset applies to the shared SuperGrok pool.
- Users can redeem usage in Settings → Usage and check the remaining balance in the Bot interface.
- Usage stats may not appear in the app when tracked separately.

## Context
Grok Bot is an interface for interacting with Grok. SuperGrok plans provide subscription-based access that includes usage allowances.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the clarification in the source post and does not include pricing, exact limits, or other plan details.
