On 21 August 2026, **Elon Musk** posted on X:

> Grok 4.6 on extra high thinking mode now achieves #1 score on CursorBench!

The post quoted @XFreeze, who published a CursorBench 3.2 comparison that listed Grok 4.6 Extra High first, alongside other named models and per-task cost figures.

## What was stated

- Elon attributed the #1 CursorBench score to **Grok 4.6** in **extra high thinking mode**
- The quoted post described the leaderboard as **CursorBench 3.2**

Grokati is reporting Elon’s statement and the existence of that quoted comparison. It is not an independent benchmark run.

## Limits of this report

No verification of CursorBench methodology, the cost-per-task figures, or later leaderboard changes is included. Those numbers belong to the quoted post, not to an xAI product announcement.
