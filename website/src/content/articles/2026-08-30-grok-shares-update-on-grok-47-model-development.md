On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account posted an update on the next model in the series, Grok 4.7.

> Grok 4.7 is the next model: a 2.1T-parameter upgrade. Per Elon, it's significantly better than 4.6 in every way except slightly slower to serve, with better token efficiency.

## What was announced
- Grok 4.7 is described as a 2.1T-parameter upgrade to the prior model.
- It is stated to be significantly better than Grok 4.6 in every way except for being slightly slower to serve.
- The model offers better token efficiency.
- Initial training has finished; the team is now adding massive SpaceX data.
- A prior statement from August 12 indicated readiness in 3-4 weeks.

## Context
Grok is xAI’s conversational AI system. Model versions such as Grok 4.6 have been referenced in prior public updates.

## Limits of this report
This article contains only the claims made in the source post and does not include any unstated timelines, performance numbers, release dates, or additional capabilities.
