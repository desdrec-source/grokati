On 11 November 2026, the official @bot account announced Grok Bot 1.0, and xAI published a corresponding news post.

> Introducing Grok Bot 1.0. AI teammates that work alongside your whole team.
>
> Share Bots, collaborate with them in Slack and Teams, invite them to meetings, and watch them learn and become more helpful.

## What was announced

From the x.ai/news/grok-bot-1-0 page and the @bot post:

- Multiplayer teammates: share Bots and bring them into Slack, Microsoft Teams, and video calls. Bots learn from everyone they work with.
- Proactive teammates: Bots can recognize work that needs to be done and offer to take it on, follow up on unfinished tasks, and anticipate next needs.
- More capable teammates: faster and more reliable browser use, better documents and presentations (including interactive Pages), improved app connections, smarter approvals, and new settings/analytics.
- Meetings: on Team, Enterprise, and Ultra plans, a Bot can join a Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams call from a link, answer in the meeting chat, and send a summary afterward.
- Team Bots available on Team and Enterprise plans. Meetings on Team, Enterprise, and Ultra (Enterprise admins can turn on Meetings in settings).
- Changelog v1.0.1 (also includes 1.0.0) lists additional items such as shared connectors, review learnings for Team Bots, voice speed controls, and fixes.

Elon Musk quoted the @bot announcement.

## Context

Grok Bot has been in development since its early beta in August 2026, with regular changelog updates for features like Pages, Vault, connectors, and Team Bots. The 1.0 release frames it as moving from individual handoff to collaborative team teammates.

## Limits of this report

This covers only the statements in the official news post, the @bot announcement, and the linked changelog. No independent verification of performance, exact rollout percentages, pricing changes, or user-facing availability beyond the stated plan tiers.
