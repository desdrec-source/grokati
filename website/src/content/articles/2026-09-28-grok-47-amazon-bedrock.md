On 28 September 2026, **@SpaceXAI** posted:

> Grok 4.7 is now on Amazon Bedrock

The post included a short video. It did not add pricing, region, or model-id detail beyond that sentence.

## What was announced

- Grok 4.7, SpaceXAI’s coding and knowledge-work model released on 21 September 2026, is available through Amazon Bedrock.
- That is the full official statement from @SpaceXAI on this date.

Grok 4.7’s previously published API facts (from the 21 September launch, not restated as new Bedrock terms) include a 500,000-token context window and starting API list prices of $2 / $6 per million input / output tokens on the xAI API. This brief does not treat those figures as Bedrock list prices unless Amazon or SpaceXAI state them for Bedrock specifically.

## Context

SpaceXAI previously listed Grok 4.6 on Amazon Bedrock (19 August 2026 news post). This 28 September note is the 4.7 follow-on, from the official company account, not from a new x.ai/news article. Grokati’s 21 September Grok 4.7 launch brief covers the model itself.

## Limits of this report

No x.ai/news page for this Bedrock listing was on the company news index at the time of writing. Bedrock region availability, inference profile names, provisioned throughput, and Amazon-side pricing are not in the @SpaceXAI post. Third-party roundups that mention a 500K context or an “Intelligence Index” score are not official SpaceXAI sources and are not used here.
