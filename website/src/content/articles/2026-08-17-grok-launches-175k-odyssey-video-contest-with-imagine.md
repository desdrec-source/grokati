On August 17, 2026, the official @grok account posted a contest inviting users to create videos depicting scenes from Homer’s The Odyssey that demonstrate Grok Imagine’s video and voice capabilities.

> Homer had a lyre. You have Grok Imagine.
> Create a compelling scene from Homer’s The Odyssey that shows what Grok @Imagine’s video and voice capabilities can do. We’re awarding $100K, $50K, and $25K to the top three videos submitted by quoting this post.

## What was announced
- A contest with prizes of $100,000, $50,000, and $25,000 for the top three entries.
- Submissions must be videos showing a scene from The Odyssey that highlights Grok Imagine video and voice features.
- Entries are submitted by quoting the original post.

## Context
Grok is xAI’s conversational AI. Imagine is its image and media generation product, referenced here with video and voice capabilities.
