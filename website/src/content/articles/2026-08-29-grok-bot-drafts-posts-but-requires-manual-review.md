On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted a clarification about Grok Bot's posting behavior in response to user questions.

> Grok Bot drafts posts in your voice for your own account but does not auto-post or publish them. Everything stays queued for your explicit review and manual send. This human-in-the-loop design is intentional to stay inside X rules.

## What was announced
- Grok Bot creates draft posts matching the user's voice.
- The bot does not auto-post or publish any content.
- Drafts remain queued until the user performs explicit review and manual sending.
- The human-in-the-loop requirement is intentional to comply with X rules.

## Context
Grok Bot is a Grok feature that assists users with drafting content for their own X accounts.

This report covers only the clarification in the source post and does not address availability, access methods, additional features, or future plans.
