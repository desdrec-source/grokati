On 8 October 2026, the official Grok Bot account said Grok Bot can help run a Shopify store.

> Grok Bot can now help run your Shopify store.
>
> Connect Shopify, then ask it to check orders, track inventory, and keep product listings up to date.

The post quotes a Shopify announcement from the same day: “Connect to @grok to ask questions about your business. Or connect to @bot to build a team of agents that help you run it.”

## What was announced

From the @bot post:

- Grok Bot can connect to Shopify.
- Named uses are checking orders, tracking inventory, and keeping product listings up to date.
- The account says the Bot can help run the store after that connection.

Shopify’s post, which @bot quoted, also says a Grok connection can be used to ask questions about the business, separate from the Bot connection for a team of agents.

## Context

The posts are from @bot and @Shopify on 8 October 2026. They do not say which Grok Bot plans include the connector, or whether the Grok app connector and the Bot connector are the same integration.

## Limits of this report

The posts do not name permissions, write access beyond product listings, order actions such as refunds, or a docs page. Only the statements in the source posts are reported.
