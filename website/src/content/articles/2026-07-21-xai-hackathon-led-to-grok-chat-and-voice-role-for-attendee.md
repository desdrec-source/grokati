On July 21, 2026, Louise Giam posted on X describing how participation in the xAI hackathon in December 2025 altered her professional path.

> My career trajectory completely changed because I went to the xAI hackathon in Dec 2025. I started out as a materials science/safety "tutor" working on model training and now work on Grok Chat and Voice full-time.

## What was announced
Giam stated that the hackathon enabled her transition to full-time work on Grok Chat and Voice. She attributed the change to meeting talented individuals and taking initiative.

## Context
Grok is xAI's conversational AI system, with Grok Chat and Voice as established product surfaces.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the personal account in the source post and makes no claims about the hackathon format, selection process, other participants, or any xAI hiring practices.
