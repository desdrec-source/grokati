On August 10, 2026, the official @grok account on X announced that the new Voice connector is now available to all users.

> Try the new Voice connector in Grok. Generate voice memos, turn daily events into a personalized podcast, or create automations for a daily brief.

## What was announced
- The Voice connector allows users to generate voice memos, convert daily events into a personalized podcast, and set up automations for a daily brief.
- The feature requires no settings or configuration.
- It is now available on iOS, Android, and Web.

## Context
Grok is an AI product from xAI that includes various connectors and voice-related features as part of its product surface.
