On August 11, 2026, xAI introduced **Grok Bot** in early beta. The product is described by the official Grok Bot account as “AI teammates that do real work for you.” According to the announcement posts, bots can sign in to a user’s tools, use them the same way a human would, and return finished work.

Elon Musk stated on X the same day that xAI would “widen the Grok Bot beta after we fix basic issues with the early beta and release Grok 4.6 later this week.” Grok 4.6 was subsequently released the following day.

### What xAI has stated about Grok Bot

- Bots are designed for real agentic tool use rather than simple chat responses.
- Early access is limited while basic reliability issues are addressed.
- The product sits alongside Grok 4.6’s focus on long-running agents and multi-step tasks.
- Official presence is maintained at the @bot account on X and related xAI channels.

### Context within the Grok product line

Grok Bot appears as a practical application layer on top of the core Grok models. It emphasizes completing work that involves external tools and accounts, which aligns with the agentic improvements highlighted in the Grok 4.6 release notes.

No independent performance numbers or broader availability timeline beyond Musk’s statement have been published by xAI at the time of this writing. All details above are drawn directly from the early-beta announcement posts and Musk’s accompanying comments.

Users interested in the beta should follow official xAI and Grok Bot channels for updates on expanded access.

*Sources: Official Grok Bot early-beta posts and Elon Musk’s statements on X, August 11, 2026.*
