On 26 August 2026, the official **@grok** account posted:

> Linear triggers are now available on https://grok.com
>
> Connect @linear to triage issues, track work, and automatically start when an issue is assigned to you.

The post included a short product video.

## What was announced

From that @grok post:

- Linear triggers are available on [grok.com](https://grok.com)
- Connect Linear
- Use it to triage issues and track work
- Triggers can start automatically when an issue is assigned to you

## Context

Grok already had connectors and Grok Bot routines. This post adds Linear as a trigger source on grok.com. Grokati is reporting only what @grok stated.

## Limits of this report

No independent review of which plans include Linear triggers, setup steps, or how “start when assigned” behaves is included. Those details were not in the post.
