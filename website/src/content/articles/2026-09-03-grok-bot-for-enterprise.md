Grok Bot for Enterprise is available today. Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers receive free usage for the next two weeks and can invite their entire organization, including people who do not already hold a seat.

> Grok Bot for Enterprise is available today.  
> It’s free for all Grok and Cursor enterprise customers for the next two weeks.  
> https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-for-enterprise

## What was announced

From the official @bot post and the accompanying x.ai news page (3 September 2026):

- Grok Bot is now available for enterprises.
- Free usage for the next two weeks for all Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers.
- Organizations can invite the whole company, including users without an existing seat.
- The release adds access, network, and audit controls intended for governing Bots at scale.
- Activation is available in the Cursor admin dashboard; sales contact is offered for further details.

Elon Musk later posted a short confirmation: “Grok @Bot Enterprise is now available.”

## Context

Grok Bot had previously expanded from early access into SuperGrok, Cursor Pro, and related plans. This announcement moves the product into an explicit enterprise tier with governance features and a short free trial window for existing enterprise customers.

## Limits of this report

This article reports only the claims made in the official @bot post, the x.ai/news page, and Elon’s confirmation. It does not include independent verification of the free-trial mechanics, exact feature differences versus non-enterprise plans, pricing after the two-week period, or availability outside the stated customer groups.
