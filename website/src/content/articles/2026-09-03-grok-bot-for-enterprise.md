Grok Bot is now available for enterprises. Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers receive free usage for the next two weeks and can invite their whole organisation, including people without an existing seat.

> Grok Bot is now available for enterprises. Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers have free usage for the next two weeks, and can invite their whole organization, including people without an existing seat.

## What was announced

- Grok Bot becomes available to enterprise customers.
- Access, network, and audit controls are added so organisations can govern Bots at scale.
- Existing Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers get free usage for two weeks.
- Those customers can invite their entire organisation, including people who do not already hold a seat.
- Activation is via the admin dashboard; further information is available through the sales contact page linked from the announcement.

## Context

Grok Bot is the product that lets users create AI teammates which run on their own cloud computers, sign into the same tools the user uses, and carry work through end-to-end. Earlier public launches expanded access to SuperGrok and Cursor paid plans. This release targets enterprise governance and wider organisational rollout.
