On 10 September 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> Grok Bot is now more powerful for sales teams.
>
> Connect your Bots to Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Clay, Granola, and other GTM tools. Stay on top of accounts, complete follow-ups, and do deep research.

A follow-up in the same thread said the company is sharing its own sales team’s Bots as installable templates, each with context, connectors, and routines, and linked to [x.ai/bot/marketplace/sales](https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/sales).

## What was announced

From that **@bot** thread:

- Grok Bot can connect to **Salesforce**, **HubSpot**, **Gong**, **Clay**, **Granola**, and other GTM tools
- Stated uses: stay on top of accounts, complete follow-ups, and do deep research
- Sales Bot templates are available to install at the marketplace link above

## Context

This is a Grok Bot connector and template announcement for sales workflows. It is separate from the 31 August Microsoft Outlook, Calendar, and OneDrive plugin post.

## Limits of this report

No independent test of each named tool, no plan gating, and no list of every GTM connector is included. Those details were not in the posts.
