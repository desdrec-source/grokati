On July 21, 2026, the official @grok account on X announced that Grok in Outlook is now available.

> Grok in Outlook is now available. Draft emails, manage your calendar, and use connectors directly from your inbox.

## What was announced
- Grok integration is now available inside Microsoft Outlook.
- Users can draft emails using Grok.
- Users can manage their calendar with Grok.
- Users can access connectors directly from the inbox.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI. The announcement references connectors, a feature previously associated with Grok product integrations.
