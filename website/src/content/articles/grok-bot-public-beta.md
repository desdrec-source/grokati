On 11 August 2026, **@grok** announced that **Grok Bot** had moved into **public beta**.

> Grok Bot is in public beta — AI teammates that can sign into tools, do real work, and return finished results.

## What Grok Bot is

xAI describes Grok Bot as more than a chat window. The public framing is:

- **Teammates**, not only answer engines
- Ability to **sign into tools**
- Capacity to **perform real work**
- Delivery of **finished results**, not only drafts or suggestions

That places Grok Bot in the agent layer of the Grok stack: systems that take actions with tools, rather than models that only generate text.

## Relationship to Grok 4.5 / 4.6 and Grok Build

The core Grok models (including **Grok 4.5** and **Grok 4.6**) provide the reasoning and generation. **Grok Build** is the harness xAI highlights for coding and long-running agentic work. **Grok Bot** is the productised teammate experience on top of that direction.

An earlier early-beta note on this site covered the first public framing. The 11 August update is the wider public-beta step, with xAI also signalling plans to expand access after fixing early issues.

## What we are not claiming

No independent evaluation of reliability, tool coverage, or success rates is included here. Capabilities are reported only as xAI and @grok stated them.
