On 22 October 2026, SpaceXAI published **Introducing the SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network**.

> Our official program for companies building products, solutions, and services with Grok for our joint prospects and customers.

## What was announced

From the official news page:

- The SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network is the official program for companies building products, solutions, and services with Grok.
- Two types of partners:
  - **Transformation Partners** help customers deploy the Grok platform across the enterprise and create tangible business value.
  - **Marketplace Partners** build the plugins and product integrations that enable the Grok product suite to work with the tools customers already depend on.
- Partners get access to the training, tools, and support they need in engagements with joint customers.
- More than 130 companies participated in the early-access phase.
- Transformation Partners are building capacity through the Forward Deployed Engineer accreditation program, with engineers trained at more than 30 organizations.
- Marketplace Partners are seeing growing adoption of their products alongside Grok.
- Partners are already helping customers in every industry and around the world, from startups to Fortune 20 companies.
- Learn more and apply at x.ai/partners.

The page includes quotes from launch partners including Bain & Company, Databricks, NVIDIA, Snowflake, Galatiq, and others.

## Context

This sits alongside earlier product announcements such as Team Bots, Grok Bot for Enterprise, and Grok 4.7. It formalises a partner path for companies that already work with or build on Grok.

## Limits of this report

The page does not list full partner eligibility criteria, pricing, or exact training timelines. Only statements on the news page are reported. Quotes from named partners are attributed to the individuals and companies on that page.
