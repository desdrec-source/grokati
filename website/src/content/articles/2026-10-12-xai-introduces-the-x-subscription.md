On 12 October 2026, xAI announced The X Subscription, a single plan that brings together Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X Premium.

> Today we’re introducing The X Subscription, which brings together Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor, and X Premium in one plan. Over the next few days, we’ll make it available to everyone globally.

## What was announced

From the official x.ai news post and the @SpaceXSI thread on X:

- One subscription covers Grok (chat, voice, image and video generation), Grok Bot (AI teammates), Cursor (local and cloud coding agents), and X Premium benefits (checkmark, creator tools, fewer or no ads depending on tier).
- Paid plans include a shared pool of Grok model usage across Grok and Cursor. Each comparable new tier provides more usage-per-dollar than the plan it replaces. The shared pool covers Grok models in the Grok apps, the Grok tab on X, or Cursor. Each X Subscription plan includes at least twice as much coding usage with xAI models as the comparable Cursor plan.
- Third-party models remain available in Cursor on Premium and above, billed separately at API list prices through top-ups.
- Five plans: Free ($0), Plus ($8/month), Premium ($30/month), Super ($100/month), Ultra ($200/month). Premium is described as the benefits of SuperGrok, Cursor Pro and X Premium+ previously $90/month combined.
- Available to new customers starting 12 October 2026. Existing SuperGrok, Cursor and X Premium subscribers can keep their current plans or switch (with prorated refund) when the option appears in billing settings. Rollout is gradual.
- Team plans at the same per-seat prices as individual plans, with additional organisation controls.
- Grok Bot is included on Premium, Super and Ultra.

## Context

Previously, SuperGrok, Cursor and X Premium were separate subscriptions. The new structure unifies billing and usage for first-party models while keeping third-party model access optional and metered.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only statements from the x.ai news page and the @SpaceXSI posts. It does not include exact weekly token amounts, which specific third-party models remain available, tax treatment, or the precise schedule for existing-subscriber migration beyond the gradual rollout noted.
