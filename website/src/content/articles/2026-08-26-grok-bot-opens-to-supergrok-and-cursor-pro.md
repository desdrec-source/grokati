On 26 August 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> All SuperGrok and Cursor Pro subscribers now have access to Grok Bot.
>
> We're also resetting weekly usage limits for all users.
>
> Enjoy!

The same day, **@mattyp** restated the change and posted a getting-started video (embedded above).

> Grok Bot is now available to all Pro & SuperGrok subscribers.
>
> Limits have also been reset. Here's a video on how to get started.

## What was announced

From **@bot**:

- Access for all SuperGrok and Cursor Pro subscribers
- Weekly usage limits reset for all users

From **@mattyp**:

- Available to Pro and SuperGrok subscribers
- Limits reset
- Getting-started video

## Context

On 21 August, @bot said Grok Bot was available to SuperGrok Plus, Cursor Pro+, and Cursor Teams, with a limited free trial for other users. This 26 August update names SuperGrok and Cursor Pro and states a weekly-limit reset.

## Limits of this report

No independent check of plan mapping, reset size, or how long the new limits last is included. Those details were not stated.
