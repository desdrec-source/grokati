On August 15, 2026, xAI user @Baconbrix posted that the team has shipped two updates in response to user feedback.

> We appreciate all the feedback! Here’s what we’ve shipped: 1. Projects for everyone, everywhere! Custom instructions and shared files across chats. 2. Grok Build tab— our most agentic harness is expanding to more users. This is Grok 4.6.

## What was announced
- Projects are now available for everyone, with custom instructions and shared files that work across chats.
- The Grok Build tab, described as the most agentic harness, is expanding to more users and is tied to Grok 4.6.

## Context
Grok is xAI’s conversational AI system. Grok 4.6 refers to a version of the underlying model. Grok Build is a product tab focused on agentic capabilities for building.
