On August 25, 2026, Lee Rob announced on X that Cursor has increased the included usage of Grok models.

> We just increased the included usage of Grok models in Cursor! Demand has been increasing with the launch of Grok 4.6. We already doubled limits last month with more compute. And now we're raising limits again permanently.

## What was announced
- Included usage of Grok models in Cursor was increased.
- The change follows rising demand after the launch of Grok 4.6.
- Limits were previously doubled last month.
- The new increase is permanent.

## Context
Grok refers to xAI's series of AI models. Cursor is a code editor that integrates multiple AI models for developer workflows.

## Limits of this report
This report contains only the details stated in the source post and does not specify new usage amounts, exact dates of prior changes, or any other product metrics.
