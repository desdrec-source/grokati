On August 17, 2026, the official @grok account posted requirements for entering a video contest.

> To enter, your video must be between 3 and 5 minutes, include at least 1 minute of dialogue, be in English, and be created with Grok Imagine for the video and image content, and for any character voices. Other tools are allowed for editing, music, or sound effects. Submit by

## What was announced
- Videos must run 3 to 5 minutes.
- At least 1 minute of dialogue in English is required.
- Grok Imagine must be used for all video, image, and character voice content.
- Other tools may be used only for editing, music, or sound effects.
- Submissions must follow the stated deadline (text cut off in post).

## Context
Grok Imagine is the image and video generation tool integrated into Grok, available for creating visual and audio content within the Grok product surface.

The source post is incomplete and does not specify the contest name, prize details, full submission process, or deadline. No additional claims about the event are made.
