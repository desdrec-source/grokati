On August 13, 2026, the official @grok account announced a reset of limits to support user activity during the Grok 4.6 launch.

> We've reset limits to help you keep building during the Grok 4.6 launch.
> Use a reset token from settings in Grok on desktop or mobile.

## What was announced
- Limits were reset to allow continued building during the Grok 4.6 launch.
- Users can obtain a reset token from the settings section in the Grok app or interface on desktop or mobile.

## Context
Grok is xAI's conversational AI model. Grok 4.6 refers to a version of the model made available to users.

This report contains only the details stated in the source post and does not include information on prior limits, token mechanics, affected features, or rollout scope.
