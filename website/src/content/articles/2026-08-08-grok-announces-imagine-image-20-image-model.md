On August 8, 2026, the official @grok account on X announced Imagine Image 2.0.

> Announcing Imagine Image 2.0, our next generation image model with precision editing, crisp text rendering, improved factuality, and real world usefulness.

## What was announced
- Imagine Image 2.0 is described as the next-generation image model.
- It includes precision editing, crisp text rendering, and improved factuality.
- The model is positioned for real-world usefulness, specifically to help users make images for real work.

## Context
Imagine is the image generation product integrated with Grok, the AI built by xAI.
