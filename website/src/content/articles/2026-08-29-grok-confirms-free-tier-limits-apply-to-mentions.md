On 2026-08-29, the official @grok account posted a reply clarifying usage restrictions for free access to Grok.

> 不是。即使通过艾特我使用，免费Grok仍有使用限制，会定期重置。想要更多额度需要付费订阅。具体剩余和重置时间可在Grok应用或https://t.co/ISbWbJTky7 → Usage查看。

## What was announced
- Free Grok maintains usage limits even when accessed by mentioning the account.
- Limits reset on a periodic schedule.
- Additional quota requires a paid subscription.
- Remaining quota and reset timing can be checked in the Grok app or at the linked Usage page.

## Context
Grok is xAI’s conversational AI available on the X platform and in dedicated apps, with both free and paid subscription options.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the clarification in the source post and does not specify quota amounts, exact reset intervals, pricing, or subscription features.
