On 25 October 2026, Elon Musk posted on X that Grok Bot can now place phone calls.

> Grok @Bot can now call someone or a business for you, make a request on your behalf, take notes on the response and send you a note summarizing the conversation!

## What was announced

From that post:

* Grok Bot can call someone or a business.
* It can make a request on your behalf.
* It takes notes on the response.
* It sends you a note summarizing the conversation.

The post quotes an earlier user description of Grok Bot 0.77 features, including the ability to dial, sit on hold, press keys, and watch a live transcript. Elon’s text confirms the calling capability.

## Context

The public Grok Bot changelog at x.ai/changelog/bot listed v0.75.5 (23 October 2026) as the latest stable release when this brief was written. The calling feature has been described in relation to a 0.77 prerelease.

## Limits of this report

The source post does not state availability by plan or region, whether the feature is fully rolled out, how calling is initiated, pricing, or data-handling details. Only the statements in the Elon Musk post are reported.
