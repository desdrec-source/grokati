On 22 October 2026, the Grok Bot changelog published v0.75.2, covering Pages, new chat cards, and video creation. It also rolls up changes from 0.72.0 through 0.75.1.

> Bots can build Pages, such as reports, memos, and dashboards, that you edit together or have the Bot keep up to date. Bots can create videos from a description or from an image you share.

## What was announced

Selected new items from the changelog:

- Bots can build Pages (reports, memos, dashboards) that you edit together or have the Bot keep up to date.
- Bots can create videos from a description or from an image you share.
- You can pin Library pages, files, and links to a Pinned section that stays at the top and syncs across devices.
- The dashboard’s Pages section lists shared Pages, where you can unshare or delete them.
- Hovering or focusing a Library row shows a preview with who sent the item, when, and who can see a Page.
- Bots can pay with Shop Pay (Shop checkout where accepted, or a one-time Shop card elsewhere).
- Connect X Money in one tap from a chat, the connector page, or checkout.
- Page owners can share a Page with their whole organization or with another team.
- Team admins can limit who can share Pages with everyone on the team and turn on link previews for Team Bots.
- Additional connector and team improvements, including GitHub commit/PR tools, Gmail attachment and reply-all support, and multi-event Google Calendar edits.

Improved items include a new chat card design, a one-column Bot details pane, voice calls that draw on earlier calls and recent chat, and better handling of signed-out connectors.

## Context

This follows earlier Bot updates such as Team Bots (public beta), IncidentWatch/IncidentSweep, and mobile local tools. Pages and video creation expand what a Bot can produce and keep current inside the product.

## Limits of this report

The changelog does not specify which plan tiers receive every item, exact video length or resolution limits, or when the features finished rolling out to all users. Only statements on the changelog page are reported.
