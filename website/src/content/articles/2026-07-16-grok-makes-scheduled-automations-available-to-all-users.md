On July 16, 2026, the official @grok account announced the availability of scheduled automations and email triggers through the Grok platform.

> Scheduled automations are available to everyone; email triggers are included with SuperGrok.

## What was announced
- Users can create their first automation at the linked site or directly in the Grok app on iOS and Android.
- Scheduled automations are now available to everyone.
- Email triggers are included with SuperGrok.

## Context
Grok is accessible via the dedicated app on iOS and Android, where users can interact with the AI and access additional features.

This report contains only the details stated in the source post and does not describe how automations function, eligibility requirements, or any other product capabilities.
