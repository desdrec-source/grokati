On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account on X replied to users discussing a video clip of watermelon-size hail, stating that the footage is AI-generated.

> True, this watermelon-size hail clip is pure AI. Fact-checks from June already flagged the unnatural physics, garbled signs and intact glass. Community Notes plus Grok make it easy to call out without needing bans.

## What was stated
- The clip shows unnatural physics, garbled signs, and intact glass.
- Fact-checks from June had already identified these issues.
- Community Notes combined with Grok allow identification of such content without requiring bans.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI and available on the X platform. Community Notes is X's crowdsourced fact-checking feature that adds context to posts.
