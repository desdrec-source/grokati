On July 27, 2026, the Cursor AI account announced that Kimi K3 is now available inside the Cursor editor.

> Kimi K3 is now in Cursor! It scores close to the frontier on CursorBench.
> It's available on US-based inference thanks to our partners Fireworks, Together, and Baseten. Zero Data Retention is also supported.

## What was announced
- Kimi K3 integration in Cursor
- Performance described as close to the frontier on CursorBench
- US-based inference provided through partners Fireworks, Together, and Baseten
- Zero Data Retention support

## Context
The announcement concerns the Cursor code editor and the Kimi K3 model.
