# Grok Seeks Android Voice Feedback from Users

On February 23, 2026, @LouiseGiam posted on X that the team had heard user requests about Grok Voice on Android and invited further suggestions.

> You asked and we heard! Let us know what else you’d like on Android for Grok Voice!

## What was announced
- The post confirms that user feedback on Grok Voice for Android has been received.
- It asks users to share additional desired features or improvements.

## Context
Grok Voice provides voice-based interaction with Grok models and is accessible via the Grok app on Android devices.

## Limits of this report
No specific features, changes, timelines, or implementation details were stated in the post.
