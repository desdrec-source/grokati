On 23 October 2026, the Grok Bot changelog published v0.75.5. It covers a Vault for credentials and contact details, email forwarding, Slack request attribution, and a Halloween pumpkin avatar option. The release also includes changes from 0.75.3 and 0.75.4.

> Bots can save your logins, cards, and contact info to a Vault in Settings and fill them in to sign in or check out. Bots can forward an email from their own inbox with its original formatting, images, and attachments.

## What was announced

New items from the changelog:

- Bots can save logins, cards, and contact info to a Vault in Settings and fill them in to sign in or check out. Requires Grok Bot 0.75.2 or later.
- Bots can forward an email from their own inbox with its original formatting, images, and attachments. Requires Grok Bot 0.75.2 or later.
- Bots record who asked for each message they post in Slack, so anyone in the thread can ask who requested it.
- For Halloween, you can give a Bot a pumpkin shape when you create it or change its avatar. Requires Grok Bot 0.75.2 or later.

Improved items include Auto-review asking less often for actions requested in the user’s own words, stopping after a denied approval card until a new message, sending requested emails and Slack messages once Auto-review allows them (while routine sends still draft), offering connector cards on sign-in handoffs, connecting X and X Money through a linked account, raising routine limits to 300 times an hour total, and clearer private/VPN blocking counts when routing through the computer.

The official @bot account posted the same day: “Grok Bot can now save your logins, cards, and contact information. Add them once, and Bot securely remembers them for the future.” Elon Musk quoted the post: “Grok @Bot saves sensitive info in a vault.”

## Context

This follows the 22 October v0.75.2 changelog that added collaborative Pages, video creation, and Shop Pay. The Vault is a native credential store inside Grok Bot Settings, distinct from earlier 1Password integration notes.

## Limits of this report

The changelog and posts do not specify encryption details, which plan tiers receive the Vault, how many items can be stored, or the exact rollout status beyond the version being listed as latest. Only statements on the changelog page and the cited posts are reported.
