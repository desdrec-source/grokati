On 17 November 2026, the official **@bot** account posted about five Grok Bots with full-time roles on the SpaceXSI accounting team.

> Five Bots now have full-time roles on our accounting team. We onboard, train, and manage them just like human teammates.
>
> Here's how we built them to handle the order-to-cash process, from provisioning customer accounts to collecting payments.
>
> https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-accounting

The linked page, published 16 November 2026 by Annay (Paul) Bains, Will Smerdon, and Erin Jenks, details the setup.

## What was announced

From the @bot post and the x.ai news page:

* Five Team Bots each own a scoped accounting job end to end.
* **Provisioning Bot** checks contracts and sets up or closes customer accounts.
* **Billing Bot** verifies contract details and generates invoices (reducing the process from roughly 24 hours to minutes).
* **Billing Support Bot** answers questions from the go-to-market team and handles routine updates (estimated to free about eight hours a day of accounting work).
* **Cash Applications Bot** applies incoming payments, investigates discrepancies, writes off bank fees, and flags short payments (work that normally takes an accountant 3–4 hours now takes roughly 30 minutes).
* **Collections Bot** handles customer-facing billing inquiries and coordinates refund requests (which still require human approval).
* SpaceXSI estimates that handling this workload without the Bots would require more than doubling the accounting team headcount.
* Bots are onboarded over about three weeks (knowledge base and observation, then proposed actions with approval, then independent routine work with escalation of exceptions). Documentation lives in Notion; Bots cannot edit it.

Elon Musk quoted the @bot post the same day: “Grok @Bot can do your accounting”.
