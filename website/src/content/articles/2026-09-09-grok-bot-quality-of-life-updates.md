On 9 September 2026, the official **@bot** account posted a thread of product updates, starting with:

> Some recent quality-of-life improvements to Grok Bot.
>
> You can ask your Bot to draft messages inline for you to approve before sending.

## What was announced

From that **@bot** thread:

- Bots can draft messages **inline** for the user to approve before sending
- Switch between Grok Bot accounts with **one click**
- The Grok Bot application is “even more performant” (quoting a 7 September staff post from @poteto on desktop cold-start and related timings)
- Users have an average of **10%** more included usage from a more efficient harness; **up to 35%** more for heavy users (quoting a 5 September staff post from @jediahkatz)
- Grok Bot is **natively available for iPad**, with an App Store link: [apps.apple.com … platform=ipad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/grok-bot/id6794501026?platform=ipad)
- Support for **over 20 languages**
- Share a file directly with Grok Bot on **mobile**

The thread closed with [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot).

## Context

These posts are incremental product notes, not a new plan tier. The usage figures are @bot restating staff posts, not an independent measurement.

## Limits of this report

The thread did not list the 20-plus languages, say whether file share is iOS-only, or publish a full performance table. Only the wording above is reported.
