On 11–12 October 2026, Tesla’s official **@robotaxi** account and Elon Musk confirmed that Grok Bot can be used during Cybercab robotaxi rides.

> Talk directly to your Grok @bot in Robotaxi

Elon Musk quoted a post detailing the feature and wrote: “Try Grok @Bot”.

## What was announced

From the official and amplified posts:

- Riders can talk directly to their Grok Bot inside a Cybercab / Robotaxi.
- Sign in to Grok once; Bots stay available on future rides in the same account.
- The session logs out automatically when the rider exits the vehicle, for privacy.
- Example tasks shown or described: adding items to an Amazon cart, sending emails, placing a coffee order, managing calendar or finances, all by voice.
- Staff and creator videos show the Grok interface active on the Cybercab display while the vehicle drives.

## Context

Grokati previously reported Grok Bot with Connectors available hands-free in Tesla vehicles on 22 September 2026, then limited to SuperGrok Heavy with more tiers later. The September note covered general Tesla vehicles. This October update specifically covers the purpose-built Cybercab robotaxi service and the sign-in / logout behaviour for shared rides.

Tesla engineer Yun-Ta Tsai stated on 11 October that Grok Bot was enabled on all Cybercabs and the rest of the Model Y fleet.

## Limits of this report

The posts do not list plan requirements, supported cities, exact software version, or which connectors are available inside the vehicle. Details of automatic logout and one-time sign-in come from the post Elon amplified, not from a Tesla engineering or privacy document. No new pricing or rate-limit changes were stated.
