## Connectors Now in Voice Mode

A post from @grok states that Connectors are now available in Voice Mode.

The update allows users to ask Grok about emails, check daily meetings, or access any existing connectors through voice interaction.

No additional details on rollout or supported connectors were provided in the announcement.
