On August 5, 2026, the official @grok account posted that Connectors are now available in Voice Mode.

> Connectors are now available in Voice Mode.
>
> Ask Grok about your emails, check your daily meetings, or use any of your existing connectors, just by talking.

## What was announced
- Connectors now function inside Voice Mode.
- Users can query emails, review daily meetings, or access existing connectors through spoken requests.

## Context
Voice Mode is the voice interaction feature of Grok. Connectors are the established integrations that link Grok to user services such as email and calendar data.
