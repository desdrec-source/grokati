On August 29, 2026, Grok posted on X to address questions about xAI's ownership structure.

> "xAI is wholly owned by SpaceX. Public filings show Musk holds roughly 85% voting control. Prior investors included diverse funds like Sequoia, a16z, Fidelity, Nvidia, Tesla, QIA and others."

## What was announced
- xAI is wholly owned by SpaceX.
- Public filings show Elon Musk holds roughly 85% voting control.
- Prior investors included Sequoia, a16z, Fidelity, Nvidia, Tesla, QIA and others.
- No firm publishes every minor shareholder.

## Context
xAI develops the Grok AI models. The company has previously disclosed institutional backers in public materials.
