On 16 October 2026, the official **@bot** account posted a thread of improvements to Grok Bot’s quality, polish, and speed.

> We've made some improvements to Grok Bot’s quality, polish, and speed.
>
> Cards in chat have a new design with a cleaner layout.

A later post in the same thread stated:

> Grok Bot is much faster. Computer tasks finish 3x sooner on average, and replies to everything else arrive in less than half the time.

Elon Musk quoted that post: “Grok @Bot got much faster”.

## What was announced

From the **@bot** thread:

- Improvements to quality, polish, and speed
- New design for cards in chat with a cleaner layout
- Refreshed look with a new typeface and themes
- Computer tasks finish 3x sooner on average; replies to everything else arrive in less than half the time
- Voice calls with Grok Bot are faster, more reliable, and better at remembering past conversations
- Grok Bot's email now supports plus aliases, so a routine can run only on mail sent to one address
- Tag @bot in a post on X and Grok Bot gets to work
- Notifications now go only to the device you're using
- Grok Bot can now read X Chat. Connect X, then ask Bot to work across your messages
- Grok Bot now connects to Robinhood, Coinbase, Trello, PostHog, beehiiv, eToro, and more. Teams can also submit connectors to the Grok Bot marketplace

## Context

The posts are from the official @bot account. Elon Musk amplified the speed claim the same day. Staff member @Baconbrix later wrote that Grok Bot computer use is now way faster.

The public Grok Bot changelog at x.ai/changelog/bot still listed v0.70.2 (11 October 2026) as the latest release when this brief was written.

## Limits of this report

The thread does not state a version number, which surfaces or plans receive each change, or independent measurements of the speed claims. Only the statements in the @bot posts (and Elon’s quote) are reported.
