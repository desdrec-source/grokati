On 19 August 2026, **xAI** published on its news site:

> Today, we’re excited to announce that Grok 4.6 is now generally available on Amazon Bedrock.

## What was announced

From [x.ai/news/grok-4-6-amazon-bedrock](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6-amazon-bedrock):

- Grok 4.6 is generally available on Amazon Bedrock
- Context window stated as **500k**
- Configurable reasoning efforts: **low**, **medium**, **high**, **xhigh**
- Listed prices:
  - Input: **$2** per 1 million tokens
  - Cached input: **$0.50** per 1 million tokens
  - Output: **$6** per 1 million tokens

The page also points to the earlier Grok 4.6 model announcement.

## Context

This is a cloud availability note for Grok 4.6 on AWS Bedrock. It is separate from the later Microsoft Foundry listing.

## Limits of this report

No independent check of Bedrock regions, quotas, or whether these prices changed after 19 August is included. Only the figures on that news page are reported.
