On 2026-08-04, @cursor_ai posted that MoK now powers training across tens of thousands of GPUs at Cursor.

> MoK now powers training across tens of thousands of GPUs at Cursor.
> In production, it raised end-to-end training throughput by 1.41x over our previous DeepEP-based stack.

## What was announced
- MoK is now used for training across tens of thousands of GPUs.
- It delivered a 1.41x improvement in end-to-end training throughput compared with the prior DeepEP-based stack.

## Context
The announcement concerns internal training infrastructure at Cursor, an AI coding tool company. No connection to Grok or xAI products is stated.
