On December 7, 2025, @LouiseGiam posted about EchoX, a project created for the xAI hackathon.

> EchoX puts X in your ears, so you never miss a beat!
> Streaming summaries of what's happening on X.

## What was announced
- EchoX delivers streaming summaries of activity on X in audio form.
- The tool was built during the xAI hackathon.
- It is intended for users who cannot always scroll X.

## Context
EchoX was developed as a participant project in an xAI hackathon.
