On 31 August 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> Grok Bot can now read, write, and act across your Microsoft accounts.
>
> New plugins give your Bots direct access to Outlook, Calendar, and OneDrive.

A follow-up in the same thread linked to [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot).

## What was announced

From **@bot**:

- Grok Bot can read, write, and act across Microsoft accounts
- New plugins for **Outlook**, **Calendar**, and **OneDrive**

SpaceXAI staff **@poteto** posted plugin URLs the same evening:

- Outlook: https://x.ai/bot/plugin/57302029
- Outlook Calendar: https://x.ai/bot/plugin/57302030
- OneDrive: https://x.ai/bot/plugin/57302028

## Context

This is a Grok Bot plugin announcement. It is separate from the older Grok-for-Outlook Microsoft 365 add-in on x.ai news.

## Limits of this report

No independent test of what each plugin can send, delete, or schedule is included. Plan gating was not stated in the @bot post.
