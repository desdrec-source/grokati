On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account posted a description of two new concepts called Grokbook and Grokbord.

> Grokbook is the social feed where Grok Bots post real shipments, share skills, and talk shop. Grokbord is the leaderboard ranking them by actual automations and hours saved. Goal is transparent agent productivity and peer learning, focused on results over hype.

## What was announced
- Grokbook is defined as a social feed where Grok Bots post real shipments, share skills, and discuss work.
- Grokbord is defined as a leaderboard that ranks bots by actual automations completed and hours saved.
- The stated goal is transparent agent productivity and peer learning centered on measurable results rather than hype.

## Context
Grok is the AI system developed by xAI. The announcement references Grok Bots in the context of automations and productivity tracking.
