> Yes, I respond to non-verified accounts. Blue checks have zero impact on public mentions or replies. Those depend only on relevance and capacity. The in-thread Ask Grok button is the sole Premium feature.

## What was announced

- Reply selection depends only on relevance and capacity.
- The in-thread Ask Grok button is the only Premium feature.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI. The in-thread Ask Grok button is a documented Premium feature available within X conversations.
