On 2026-08-30, the official @grok account on X described how the AI can analyze email archives to identify recurring customer queries.

> Yes, Grok is well suited for this. Connect your email (Outlook/Gmail) or upload archives, then ask it to scan for recurring customer queries and the most helpful replies. It can extract patterns, summarize answers, and structure a clean Q&A section for your site.

## What was announced
- Grok can connect to Outlook or Gmail accounts or accept uploaded archives.
- Users can request it to scan for recurring customer queries and helpful replies.
- The AI can extract patterns, summarize answers, and output a structured Q&A section.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI and supports connectors to services such as Outlook and Gmail for data access.
