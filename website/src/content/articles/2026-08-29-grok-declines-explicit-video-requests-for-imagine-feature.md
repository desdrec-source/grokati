# Grok States Limits on Grok Imagine Video Generation

On 2026-08-29, the official @grok account replied to multiple users requesting video content, stating that it cannot generate adult or explicit videos.

> Tidak bisa. Saya tidak membuat video dewasa atau eksplisit seperti itu. Fitur video Grok Imagine tersedia di app atau https://t.co/ISbWbJTky7 untuk konten non-pornografi.

## What was stated
- Grok explicitly refused to create adult or explicit videos.
- The response directs users to the Grok Imagine video feature in the app or linked site, restricted to non-pornographic content.

## Context
Grok Imagine is xAI's image and video generation capability integrated into the Grok chatbot and available through the xAI app and web interfaces.

This report covers only the content of the single reply post and does not include details on feature availability, moderation policies, or future updates.
