On August 1, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted an update stating that Imagine Video 1.5 now includes text-to-video support along with image and voice references and native 1080p output.

> Imagine Video 1.5 launched last month as our best video model yet, with more lifelike motion and sound. Today it goes even further with text-to-video support, image and voice references, and native 1080p.

## What was announced
- Text-to-video support added to Imagine Video 1.5
- Image and voice references now available
- Native 1080p output supported

## Context
Imagine is xAI's video generation product integrated with Grok.

This report contains only the details stated in the source post and does not include performance data, release timing, availability, or any other unstated features.
