On August 1, 2026, the official @grok account posted an update on X stating that Imagine Video 1.5 has received additional features.

> Imagine Video 1.5 launched last month as our best video model yet, with more lifelike motion and sound. Today it goes even further with text-to-video support, image and voice references, and native 1080p.

## What was announced
- Text-to-video support added to Imagine Video 1.5.
- Image and voice references now supported.
- Native 1080p output introduced.

## Context
Imagine is xAI’s video generation model integrated with Grok.

## Limits of this report
This article reports only the claims made in the August 1 post and does not include performance data, release timing, availability, or any other details.
