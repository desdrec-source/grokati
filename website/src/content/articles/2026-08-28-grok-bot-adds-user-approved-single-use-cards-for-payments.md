On August 28, 2026, the account @bot posted an update describing a new payment workflow for Grok Bot.

> You approve every spend request, and Grok Bot receives a secure, single-use card for each payment.
> Currently available to users in the US, rolling out to mobile soon.

## What was announced
- Every spend request requires explicit user approval.
- Grok Bot is issued a secure, single-use card for each approved payment.
- The feature is currently available to users in the US.
- A mobile rollout is planned.

## Context
Grok Bot is a named product in the Grok and xAI surface that can perform automated tasks including payments.

## Limits of this report
No further details on card issuance mechanics, security implementation, supported merchants, or timelines beyond the stated US availability and mobile rollout are provided in the source.
