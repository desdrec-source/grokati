On 17 September 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> Grok Bot can talk now.

A follow-up in the same thread said:

> Voice is rolling out on desktop and mobile over the next couple of days.
>
> Download Grok Bot: https://x.ai/bot

The source post included a product video (embedded above).

## What was announced

From that **@bot** thread:

- Grok Bot can talk
- Voice is rolling out on **desktop and mobile** over the next couple of days
- Download link: [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot)

## Context

This is a Grok Bot voice rollout note from the official Bot account. It does not replace earlier Grok Voice or connector posts.

## Limits of this report

No list of languages, voices, plan gating, or which Bot types get voice is included. Those details were not in the posts.
