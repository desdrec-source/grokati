> Grok 4.6 is here! 🚀 this has been my daily driver for over a week, no going back! 
> Try it now in Grok Build, the API, and Cursor!

## What was announced
- Grok 4.6 is accessible in Grok Build.
- Grok 4.6 is accessible via the API.
- Grok 4.6 is accessible in Cursor.
- The poster stated they have used it as their daily driver for over a week.

Grok refers to the family of large language models developed by xAI. Grok Build is one of the interfaces where users can interact with Grok models, alongside the public API and third-party tools such as Cursor.
