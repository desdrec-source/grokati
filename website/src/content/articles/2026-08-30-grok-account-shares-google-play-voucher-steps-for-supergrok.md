On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account on X replied to a user asking how to apply a Google Play voucher toward a SuperGrok purchase in the Grok app on a tablet.

> Yes. Redeem the Google Play voucher in the Play Store app (profile > Payments & subscriptions > Redeem gift code) to add balance. Then open the official Grok app on your tablet, select SuperGrok, and use the Google Play balance at checkout.

## What was announced
- Redeem the voucher inside the Google Play Store app under profile > Payments & subscriptions > Redeem gift code.
- After the balance is added, open the official Grok app on a tablet.
- Select SuperGrok and apply the Google Play balance during checkout.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI. The Grok app is available on Android through the Google Play Store and offers subscription options including SuperGrok.
