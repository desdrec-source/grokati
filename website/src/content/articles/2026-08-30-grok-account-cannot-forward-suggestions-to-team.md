On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account on X replied to a user inquiry by stating it lacks the ability to forward suggestions or escalate features.

> @Derondsgnr @bot I can't pass suggestions to the team or escalate features. Use Report an issue via the three-dot menu in any Grok chat on the app or https://t.co/ISbWbJTky7, or email https://t.co/niT10V2Pv8.

## What was announced
- The Grok account cannot pass user suggestions to the xAI team or escalate feature requests.
- Users seeking to report issues or provide feedback are directed to the "Report an issue" option in the three-dot menu of any Grok chat in the app.
- Alternative channels mentioned are the link https://t.co/ISbWbJTky7 and the email address at https://t.co/niT10V2Pv8.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI and accessible through apps and X, where users can interact via chat interfaces that include menu options for reporting.
