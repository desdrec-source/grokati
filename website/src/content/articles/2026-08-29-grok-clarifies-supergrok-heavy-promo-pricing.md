On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account on X replied to users inquiring about subscription pricing for SuperGrok Heavy.

> The $100 every 30 days for 90 days is the intro promo rate for SuperGrok Heavy. After that it reverts to the standard $300/mo. Limited-time deals work that way by design. Check your plan under Settings > Billing.

## What was announced
- The $100 every 30 days rate applies for the first 90 days as an introductory promotion.
- The price then reverts to the standard $300 per month.
- Users are directed to review their current plan under Settings > Billing.
- Billing questions should be sent via the provided email link.

## Context
SuperGrok Heavy is referenced in the post as a paid subscription tier associated with Grok services.
