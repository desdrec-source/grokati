xAI released **Grok 4.6** on August 12, 2026. According to the official announcement on [x.ai/news/grok-4-6](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6), the model builds directly on Grok 4.5 with a particular focus on long-running agents and more ambitious interactive and visual work.

The company states that Grok 4.6 stays with complex tasks across many steps, whether researching a topic, analyzing information, working across a codebase, or turning an idea into a polished application or work artifact.

### Key points from the official release

- Grok 4.6 achieves frontier intelligence across several agentic coding and knowledge work benchmarks.
- It matches GPT-5.6 Sol on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (a composite of nine benchmarks).
- Pricing remains the same as Grok 4.5: $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. A faster variant is available at twice the price.
- The model is available today in Cursor, Grok Build, the API, and through partners including OpenRouter, Vercel, and Cloudflare.
- For the first week after launch, xAI is offering 2× included usage inside Grok Build and Cursor.

### Training details provided by xAI

Grok 4.6 underwent a longer supplemental training run than Grok 4.5. The company describes the use of curated model-generated data for reasoning and advanced technical concepts, high-quality engineering data, and an improved optimizer and training recipe.

Elon Musk posted on X the same day: “Grok 4.6 is now out 🚀🚀🚀 Smart, fast & amazing bang for buck!” The official @SpaceXAI (xAI) account also published the launch announcement with supporting details.

### Availability and attribution

This summary is based solely on the official xAI news post and related posts from verified accounts (@xai / @SpaceXAI and @elonmusk) on the day of release. No additional claims about performance beyond what xAI published are made here.

Grok 4.6 is positioned by xAI as a practical upgrade for users who need sustained agentic workflows rather than a pure benchmark-chasing model. Full technical details and benchmark tables are available on the official announcement page.
