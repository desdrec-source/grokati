On July 28, 2026, the official @grok account announced that SuperGrok Heavy users can now build apps from the web, iOS, and Android.

> Build apps from https://t.co/f3u3RwhHjk, iOS, and Android. With one prompt, turn an idea into a published product with its own domain.

- App building is available from the web, iOS, and Android.
- A single prompt converts an idea into a published product that includes its own domain.
- The feature is restricted to SuperGrok Heavy users.
