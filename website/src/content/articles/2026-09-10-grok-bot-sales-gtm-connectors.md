On 10 September 2026 the official **@bot** account stated that Grok Bot is now more powerful for sales teams.

> Grok Bot is now more powerful for sales teams.
>
> Connect your Bots to Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Clay, Granola, and other GTM tools. Stay on top of accounts, complete follow-ups, and do deep research.

A follow-up post in the same thread added:

> We’re also sharing our own sales team’s Bots as templates you can install.
>
> Each one comes with context, connectors, and routines to get you started.
>
> https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/sales

## What was announced

From the **@bot** posts:

- Connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Clay, Granola and other GTM tools
- Ability to stay on top of accounts, complete follow-ups and perform deep research
- Installable templates drawn from SpaceXAI’s own sales team Bots
- Templates include context, connectors and routines
- Marketplace link: https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/sales

Elon Musk later quoted the main post and wrote “Grok @Bot is great for sales teams!”

## Context

Grok Bot is the persistent AI teammate product that can sign into tools and return finished work. Earlier September posts had already covered the public marketplace and the Haggle Bot procurement template. This update specifically expands GTM / sales connectors and ships ready-made sales templates.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the claims made in the official @bot posts and the subsequent Elon Musk quote. No independent verification of connector scope, rate limits, plan availability or template contents is included.
