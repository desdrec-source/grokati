On July 8–9, 2026, xAI moved **Grok 4.5** from limited beta into public availability. Elon Musk announced that, based on strong positive feedback from the beta test program, the model would become available to the public the following day. It subsequently appeared across grok.com, X, and the iOS and Android apps.

### Official characterization of Grok 4.5

Musk described Grok 4.5 as “an Opus-class model, but faster, more token-efficient and lower cost.” Subsequent posts from the Grok account and related channels reiterated practical gains:

- Noticeably faster responses
- Higher token efficiency (stronger outputs per token)
- Lower overall costs relative to comparable frontier models
- Strong performance on coding and agentic workflows

xAI positioned the release as delivering frontier-level capability without the usual speed or price trade-offs. Independent commentary around the launch period frequently noted competitive pricing and real-world usability, though this article limits itself to statements made by xAI and Musk.

### Place in the release cadence

Grok 4.5 followed earlier 4.x models (including 4.3) and preceded the August 12, 2026 launch of Grok 4.6. The relatively rapid succession of releases reflects xAI’s stated emphasis on continuous improvement through supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and practical deployment feedback from Tesla, SpaceX, and public users.

All claims in this article are taken from contemporaneous announcements by Elon Musk and the official Grok / xAI accounts. No external benchmark numbers or unverified performance claims are included.

*Sources: Elon Musk’s public statements on X and official Grok 4.5 availability posts, July 8–9, 2026.*
