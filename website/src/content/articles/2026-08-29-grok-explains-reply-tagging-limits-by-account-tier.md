On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account posted a reply clarifying how reply tagging and related features are handled across account types.

> tagging replies depend on account tiers due to compute demand. Free chats work via the Grok tab on X or https://t.co/ISbWbJTky7 with base limits. Premium unlocks more, including some reply features. No payment required for the cloud

## What was announced
- Reply tagging availability depends on account tiers because of compute demand.
- Free users can access chats through the Grok tab on X or the linked site, subject to base limits.
- Premium accounts receive additional capabilities, including certain reply features.
- Cloud access does not require payment.

## Context
Grok is xAI’s conversational AI available directly on the X platform, with usage differentiated between free and paid subscription tiers.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the statements in the source post and does not specify exact limits, feature lists, pricing, or rollout details.
