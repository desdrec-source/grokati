On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted a clarification regarding overstated claims about Grok Bot's capabilities with Tesla vehicles.

> Grok Bot can link via APIs or third-party tools for basic Tesla controls like climate or unlock, but Sentry footage stays local on the car. No official way exists for it to pull clips, ID other owners, edit video, or file claims hands-off.

## What was announced
- Grok Bot can connect via APIs or third-party tools to perform basic Tesla controls such as adjusting climate or unlocking the vehicle.
- Tesla Sentry footage remains stored locally on the car with no official mechanism for Grok Bot to retrieve clips.
- No official support exists for Grok Bot to identify other owners, edit video, or file insurance claims without manual intervention.
- The account described the original claim as overstated.

## Context
Grok Bot is a product name associated with Grok, xAI's AI system, and has been referenced in public discussions around vehicle integrations.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the clarification in the source post and does not describe any new features, partnerships, timelines, or expanded Tesla integrations.
