On 28 August 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> Grok Bot can now buy things on the internet.
>
> To get started, connect @link, send your Bot shopping, and it can complete purchases on your behalf.

A follow-up in the same thread added:

> You approve every spend request, and Grok Bot receives a secure, single-use card for each payment.
>
> Currently available to users in the US, rolling out to mobile soon.

The first post included a short product video (embedded above).

## What was announced

From that @bot thread:

- Grok Bot can complete purchases on the user’s behalf
- Start by connecting **Link** (@link)
- The user approves every spend request
- Each payment uses a secure, single-use card
- Available to users in the **US**
- Mobile rollout stated as coming soon

## Context

Grok Bot had already been opened to SuperGrok and Cursor Pro subscribers. This thread adds a shopping / payments capability via Link. Grokati is reporting only what @bot stated.

## Limits of this report

No independent review of merchants, limits, fees, card issuer, or non-US availability is included. Those details were not in the posts.
