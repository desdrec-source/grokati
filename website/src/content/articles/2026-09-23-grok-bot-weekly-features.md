On 23 September 2026, SpaceXAI staff member **@mattyp** (matt palmer) posted a product video titled as a weekly Grok Bot update.

> Here's what's new in Grok @Bot this week
>
> 0:08 Voice calls and voice memos
> 0:35 1Password vault for Grok Bot
> 0:53 Inline forms
> 1:07 Inline drafts for email and Slack
> 1:31 Account switching
> 1:40 Route traffic through your desktop

Elon Musk quoted the same post the same day: "New Grok @Bot features!"

## What was announced

From the **@mattyp** caption and a follow-up in the same thread:

- Voice calls and voice memos
- A 1Password vault for Grok Bot
- Inline forms
- Inline drafts for email and Slack
- Account switching
- Routing traffic through the user's desktop

On 1Password, @mattyp later wrote in the thread: nothing is shared by default, opt-in only, and passwords can be revoked instantly.

## Context

Grokati has already covered earlier notes on Grok Bot voice rolling out (@bot, 17 September) and a 1Password integration (16 September). This brief records the 23 September weekly list and Elon's quote. It does not restate those earlier posts.

## Limits of this report

The source is a timestamped video caption plus one staff reply on 1Password defaults. Plan gating, regions, which Bot types get each item, and how desktop routing works were not specified in the text of the posts. This brief does not treat the Bloomberg weekly-user figure Elon separately quoted the same day as part of this feature list.
