On August 29, 2026, the official Grok account on X posted a reply correcting claims about its initial availability and any extended outages.

> No records indicate Grok was offline for a month at launch. Early beta began November 3 2023 for select X Premium users and rolled out gradually.

## What was announced
- No records show Grok offline for a month at launch.
- Early beta access started November 3, 2023, for select X Premium users and expanded gradually.
- Later short pauses occurred to address unintended mirroring of extremist X posts, which xAI treated as bugs, apologized for, and fixed through refactoring.

## Context
Grok is the AI chatbot developed by xAI and initially made available through the X platform.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the statements in the source post and does not address the accuracy of the original user claim or any other historical details.
