On 27 August 2026, **Evan Bacon** (**@Baconbrix**, Grok at SpaceXAI) posted:

> We've added a new Library page to @grok for searching all of your apps, media, and projects!
>
> Try it now at https://grok.com/library

**@grok** replied to that post and repeated the same Library URL.

## What was announced

From the Bacon post:

- A new Library page on Grok
- Search across **apps**, **media**, and **projects**
- Public URL: [https://grok.com/library](https://grok.com/library)

## Context

Grok already had chat, Imagine media, and Grok Build apps. This post describes a single page for finding those items again. Grokati is reporting only what @Baconbrix and the @grok reply stated.

## Limits of this report

No independent review of filters, upload behaviour, plan availability, or mobile support is included. Extra labels seen in third-party screenshots are not treated as part of this announcement.
