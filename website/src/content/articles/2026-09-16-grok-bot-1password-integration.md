On 16 September 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> Grok Bot can now use 1Password.
>
> Share items in a vault, approve each fill, and the secrets stay in your password manager.

A follow-up in the same thread stated:

> We’re partnering with 1Password to help Bots securely and easily manage their own credentials.
>
> More improvements coming soon.
>
> Try Grok Bot here: https://x.ai/bot

## What was announced

From the **@bot** posts:

- Grok Bot can use 1Password.
- Users share items in a vault; each fill requires approval.
- Secrets remain in the password manager.
- The arrangement is described as a partnership with 1Password to let Bots manage their own credentials securely.

## Context

This builds on the earlier 8 September announcement that Grok Bot could fill forms and logins in chat with support for any password manager. The 16 September posts name 1Password specifically and frame it as a partnership for Bot credential management.

## Limits of this report

No further technical details (supported platforms, vault configuration steps, or enterprise controls) appear in the source posts. This report does not claim behaviour beyond what **@bot** stated.
