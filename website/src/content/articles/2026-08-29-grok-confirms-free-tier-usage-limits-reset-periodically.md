On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account posted a reply clarifying usage rules for the free version of Grok.

> 不是。免费Grok有使用限制，会定期重置。想更多额度需付费订阅。具体剩余和重置时间在Grok应用或https://t.co/ISbWbJTky7的Settings → Usage查看。

## What was announced
- Free Grok includes usage limits that reset on a regular schedule.
- Additional quota requires a paid subscription.
- Remaining usage amounts and reset times are shown in the Grok app or at the linked settings page under Settings → Usage.

## Context
Grok is the AI chatbot developed by xAI and offered in both free and subscription tiers through the Grok app and related interfaces.

## Limits of this report
This report is limited to the clarification in the source post and does not specify quota amounts, reset intervals, subscription pricing, or any other product details.
