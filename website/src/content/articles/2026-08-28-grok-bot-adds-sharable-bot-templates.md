On 28 August 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> You can now share templates of your Bots with others.

A follow-up in the same thread said:

> To get started, update to the latest Grok Bot desktop and mobile app.
>
> Download Grok Bot: https://x.ai/bot

The first post included a short product video (embedded above).

## What was announced

From that @bot thread:

- Users can share templates of their Bots with others
- Start by updating the latest Grok Bot desktop and mobile app
- Download link stated as [https://x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot)

## Context

The same day, @bot also announced that Grok Bot can complete purchases via Link. This thread is a separate product note about sharing Bot templates. Grokati is reporting only what @bot stated.

## Limits of this report

No independent review of how sharing works, what a template includes, or which plans can share is included. Those details were not in the posts.
