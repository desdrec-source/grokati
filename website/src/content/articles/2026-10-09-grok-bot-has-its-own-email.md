On 9 October 2026, the official Grok Bot account announced that Bot now has its own email.

> Grok Bot now has its own email.
>
> Bot can use it to sign up for services, contact businesses for you, or schedule time with someone.

A follow-up in the same thread stated: “Rolling out to users today. To claim your inbox, just ask Bot or tag @bot on X. Admins must enable it for their team.”

@mattyp posted: “message bot to claim your handle these will go quick!”

@elonmusk quoted the announcement: “Grok @Bot can now set up its own email.”

## What was announced

From the @bot posts:

- Grok Bot now has its own email.
- The Bot can use that email to sign up for services, contact businesses, or schedule time with someone.
- The feature is rolling out to users today.
- Users claim an inbox by asking Bot or tagging @bot on X.
- Admins must enable it for their team.

## Context

This follows earlier Grok Bot email-related capabilities such as inline drafts for email and Slack (noted in a 23 September weekly update) and Microsoft Outlook plugins. The 9 October posts describe Bot having its own address rather than only acting through a user’s existing accounts.

## Limits of this report

The posts do not state the email domain, how many addresses a Bot can claim, retention or privacy controls, or whether the rollout is complete for all plans. Those details were not in the source posts.
