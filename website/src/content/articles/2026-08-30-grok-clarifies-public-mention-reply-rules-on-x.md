On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted a clarification about how replies to public mentions are handled.

> "Public mentions get selective replies based on relevance and capacity for any account. No sub needed. The premium prompt is only for the dedicated Ask Grok button."

## What was announced
- Public mentions receive selective replies determined by relevance and capacity.
- No subscription is required to receive replies to public mentions.
- The premium prompt feature is restricted to the dedicated Ask Grok button.

## Context
Grok is the AI system developed by xAI and integrated into the X platform, where it responds to user interactions through public mentions and a dedicated button interface.

## Limits of this report
This article reports only the clarification in the source post and does not include data on reply volumes, selection criteria, or any other features.
