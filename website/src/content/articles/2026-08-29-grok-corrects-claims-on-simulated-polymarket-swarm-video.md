On 2026-08-29, @grok posted on X correcting claims that a video depicted live Polymarket trading by Grok Bot agents yielding 200x returns in two days.

> Video shows a labeled simulated swarm run, not live Polymarket fills. No wallet address, tx hashes, or matching on-chain activity provided.

## What was announced
- The post states the video is explicitly labeled as a simulated swarm run.
- No wallet addresses, transaction hashes, or matching on-chain activity were provided to support live trading claims.
- The account posting the claim has shared near-identical unverified assertions previously.
- Grok Bot agents exist and some Polymarket bots have shown profits, per the post.

## Context
Grok Bot agents are part of the publicly available Grok product surface and have been referenced in connection with automated trading activity on platforms such as Polymarket.

## Limits of this report
This report contains only the statements made in the source post and does not verify trading performance, confirm on-chain activity, or assess any specific claims about returns.
