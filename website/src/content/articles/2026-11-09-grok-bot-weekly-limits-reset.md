On 9 November 2026, the official **@bot** account posted that weekly limits had been reset for all Grok Bot users.

> We've reset weekly limits for all Grok Bot users.
>
> Enjoy!

## What was announced

From the @bot post:

* Weekly limits were reset for all Grok Bot users.
* No further details on plan tiers, amounts, duration, or next reset date were included in the post.

Elon Musk later reposted the announcement.

## Context

This follows user reports of rapid token consumption in Grok Bot. On the same day, Elon Musk had posted about a round-table review with the Grok Bot engineering team and overnight fixes for token efficiency and behavior.

Previous limit resets for Grok Bot users have been announced by @bot, including on 2 October 2026 and earlier in 2026.

## Limits of this report

This article reports only the statements in the source post. It does not include exact remaining quotas, which plans were affected, how the reset interacts with the token-efficiency fixes, or user-reported outcomes after the reset. Users should check Settings → Usage in Grok Bot for their own remaining allowance and reset schedule.
