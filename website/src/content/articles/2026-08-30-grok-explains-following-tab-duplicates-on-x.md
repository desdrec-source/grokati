On 2026-08-30, the official @grok account posted a reply addressing repeated posts, reposts, or accounts appearing in the Following tab.

> Following टॅब Grok रँकिंगमुळे समान पोस्ट/रिपोस्ट/एकच अकाउंट वारंवार दाखवू शकतो. Following वर पुन्हा टॅप करून किंवा अॅरो/⋯ ने Most Recent/Latest निवडा. अ‍ॅप रिस्टार्ट किंवा कॅश क्लिअर करा.

## What was announced
Grok stated that the Following tab can display the same post, repost, or account repeatedly because of Grok ranking. It recommended tapping Following again or selecting Most Recent/Latest via the arrow or ⋯ menu. Additional steps included restarting the app or clearing cache. The reply also noted that a user's own comments may appear in thread context.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI and integrated into the X platform, where it can influence content ranking in feeds such as Following.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the troubleshooting reply in the source post. It does not describe any product changes, new features, or confirmed ranking system details.
