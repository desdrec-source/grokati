On 28 September 2026, **@bot** announced Team Bots and pointed to the official page [Team Bots: AI coworkers that learn from your team](https://x.ai/news/team-bots).

> Introducing Team Bots, shared AI teammates that learn as your team works with them.
>
> Give your Team Bot the skills, plugins, and credentials it needs for its role, then work with it in Slack or Grok Bot.

A follow-up in the same thread said Team Bots are in public beta for Teams and Enterprise, and that SpaceXAI uses them to prep account teams each morning, coordinate engineering work, answer data questions, triage customer feedback, and run hiring loops.

## What was announced

From the official news page and the @bot thread:

- A Team Bot is built around a shared role or workflow. Everyone on the team gets the same Bot and its expertise, while still working with it individually.
- Four building blocks are listed: **context** (files, instructions, skills), **plugins** (apps such as Salesforce, Notion, and GitHub), **credentials** (third-party APIs without a plugin), and **memories** (what it learns in the role over time).
- Conversations with a shared Bot stay private per person. The Bot keeps separate context and memories for each user while drawing on skills shared across the team.
- Teams can work with a Team Bot in Slack. Each Team Bot has its own handle and can be invited to a channel.
- SpaceXAI describes internal uses in sales and customer success (per-account Bots that post weekday morning Slack briefs), product and engineering (project Slack plus Notion, Linear, Hex, Datadog, and Cursor), marketing (brand review and website/SEO changes after sign-off), and data analytics (read-only warehouse queries via Databricks plus Datadog, Hex, and Statsig).
- The engineering example says SpaceXAI used this setup while building Team Bots: a Bot steered a Cursor Project that orchestrated hundreds of Cloud Agents, helping a five-person team ship more than 100 PRs a day.
- The page also quotes two outside customers (Harper and Amplitude) describing their own Team Bot work. Those quotes are the companies’ statements as published by SpaceXAI, not independently verified here.
- Availability stated by @bot: public beta for Teams and Enterprise.

## Context

This is a product launch on x.ai/news, not a model release. Grokati has already covered Grok Bot itself, enterprise access, and the customer-support case study. Those earlier notes are not restated here.

Staff posts from @mattyp and @Baconbrix on the same day repeated the shared-Slack framing. They add colour, not extra product facts beyond the official page.

## Limits of this report

Plan names beyond “Teams and Enterprise,” pricing, seat limits, and which plugins ship on day one are not specified in the news page or the @bot thread. Internal throughput claims (100 PRs a day, hundreds of Cloud Agents) and customer savings quotes are SpaceXAI’s published figures. This brief does not independently verify them.
