On 27 October 2026, SpaceXSI published **Your next sales teammate should be a Grok Bot**.

> The SpaceXSI Sales team built Shared Bots for prospecting, quoting, and deal review so every rep works from the same up-to-date expertise.

## What was announced

From the official news page:

* The Sales team created Shared Bots that specialise in roles already present in the organisation. People who know each job best help build and maintain the Bot so everyone works from the same knowledge.
* Three Shared Bots for the deal cycle:
  * **Account Intel Bot** helps reps identify prospects, prepare for customer conversations, and keep Salesforce up to date. It can prepare briefings before meetings, read Gong transcripts afterward, make Salesforce updates, and draft follow-up emails. Reps can schedule these workflows.
  * **Quote Bot** generates pricing proposals and order forms. It inputs quotes in Salesforce, presents them side by side in the Grok Bot app, submits for approval, tracks status, generates order forms in Ironclad, and drafts customer emails. It shows a preview and asks for confirmation before changing Salesforce, and operates with the rep’s permissions.
  * **Deal Bot** supports the Deal Desk on non-standard terms and approvals. It checks requests against approved policies, prepares changes, monitors and triages Slack requests, and routes exceptions. It has permissions sales reps do not, and asks a specialist to approve decisions that require human judgment. Every Salesforce approval it processes is stamped with the decision, rationale, and DRI.
* Shared Bots act with each user’s permissions, have additional restrictions on sensitive actions, and include rate limits. Human review is required for customer-facing communications and decisions whose criteria are not clearly defined.
* Additional Shared Bots mentioned include Competitive Intel Bot and Sales Training Bot.

## Context

This is an internal SpaceXSI case study on using Shared Bots (previously covered in the Team Bots public-beta announcement) inside the sales organisation. It sits alongside earlier Grok Bot write-ups on support, procurement, and incidents.
