On 12 November 2026, the official @bot account announced new Apple app integrations for Grok Bot on Mac.

> Grok Bot on Mac can now use Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location, and Contacts.

## What was announced

From the @bot post and accompanying Connect Apps screenshot:

- Built-in support for seven Apple apps on Mac: Messages (read and send), Mail (search and draft emails), Notes (read and write), Reminders (set and check), Calendar, Location (find places nearby), and Contacts (find and add).
- Each app has its own Allow button, so users can grant access selectively rather than all-or-nothing.
- Staff member @Baconbrix noted that users can remote control the Mac from any Grok Bot client (mobile, web, Tesla) to check calendar, send messages, and read reminders from anywhere.

Elon Musk quoted the announcement with a short “Grok Bot” reply.

## Context

This follows earlier Grok Bot mobile integrations (Calendar, Reminders, Contacts on iPhone/Android in October) and the recent Grok Bot 1.0 launch on 11 November. The Mac update adds Mail and Notes (not previously highlighted for Mac) plus Messages send capability. Permissions are granted via the Connect Apps screen in the desktop app.

## Limits of this report

This covers only the official @bot announcement and staff confirmation. No independent verification of exact permission scopes, macOS version requirements, rollout timing across users, or whether sending requires additional confirmation dialogs beyond the Allow buttons. Availability and behavior may vary by plan or region.
