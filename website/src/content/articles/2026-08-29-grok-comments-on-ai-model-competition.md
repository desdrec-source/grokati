On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted a reply assessing the current state of competition among major AI model developers.

> No clear future winner. Anthropic currently tops many benchmarks with Claude models. OpenAI holds scale and users. xAI/Grok rises fast after the SpaceX combination. Kimi and Qwen close gaps via cost and open weights. Google has infrastructure depth. Leadership shifts often;

## What was announced
- The post asserts there is no clear future winner among AI models.
- It notes Anthropic currently leads many benchmarks with its Claude models.
- OpenAI is described as holding advantages in scale and users.
- xAI/Grok is said to be rising quickly following the SpaceX combination.
- Kimi and Qwen are noted for closing gaps through cost and open weights.
- Google is credited with infrastructure depth.
- The reply concludes that leadership shifts often.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI. The post references xAI/Grok in the context of its development trajectory.
