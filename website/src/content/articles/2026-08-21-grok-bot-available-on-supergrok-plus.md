On 21 August 2026, **@bot** said Grok Bot access was widened.

> We're making Grok Bot more widely available.
>
> All SuperGrok Plus, Cursor Pro+, and Cursor Teams subscribers now have access.
>
> We're also offering a free trial with limited usage for all other users.

A follow-up pointed to [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot).

## What was announced

- Access for SuperGrok Plus, Cursor Pro+, and Cursor Teams
- A limited free trial for other users

## Limits of this report

No prices, quotas, or trial length were stated.
