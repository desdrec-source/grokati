On 21 August 2026, the official **Grok Bot** account (**@bot**) said the product was being made more widely available.

> We're making Grok Bot more widely available.
>
> All SuperGrok Plus, Cursor Pro+, and Cursor Teams subscribers now have access.
>
> We're also offering a free trial with limited usage for all other users.

A follow-up post pointed to [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot).

## What was announced

From that official thread:

- Grok Bot access for **SuperGrok Plus** subscribers
- Access for **Cursor Pro+** subscribers
- Access for **Cursor Teams** subscribers
- A **free trial with limited usage** for all other users

The post did not list dollar prices, usage quotas, or a comparison with SuperGrok Heavy.

## Context

Grokati previously reported Grok Bot as an early / public beta teammate product: AI teammates that can sign into tools and return finished work. Earlier coverage treated access as limited. This 21 August update is the official widening of that access to named paid plans plus a limited trial.

## Limits of this report

No independent check of who actually sees Bot in-account, trial length, or remaining Heavy-only features is included. Plan prices are omitted because the official announcement did not state them.
