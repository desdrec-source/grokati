On 22 September 2026, SpaceXAI published [How SpaceXAI is using Grok Bot to scale customer support](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-customer-support).

> We rebuilt the combined SpaceXAI and Cursor support operation around Grok Bot, expanding to a much broader product portfolio without adding headcount.

## What was announced

From that official news page:

- After Cursor became part of SpaceXAI on 14 August, the two support teams combined around a broader product set while Grok Bot was being prepared for launch.
- The combined team saw a **175% increase in support tickets** and did not hire additional people. The page says they might have hired 200 additional people otherwise.
- Traditional AI support tools are described as charging a flat $1 to $4 per resolution. SpaceXAI says Grok Bot usage is already included in the plan, and with minor optimizations tickets have been resolved for as low as **$0.20 to $0.30**.
- Rollout used a crawl-walk-run path: first connected to Plain (ticketing) and Linear (issue tracking), limited to internal notes with human approval on every write, then traces and evaluations, then the least complex tickets, then direct customer replies.
- Grok Bot is applied as a pre-investigation step on inbound tickets. It can add to or create Linear issues, connect to Datadog, and reproduce issues with video for engineering.
- The page says the Bot was trained on over one million customer interactions for tone and voice, is instructed to push tickets toward resolution, and has refund instructions that resolve 99% of refund requests without human intervention.
- Queue work includes reprioritising tickets, reassigning ownership, SLA alerts, automatic incident declaration at volume thresholds, and monitoring X for sentiment and recurring reports.
- Improvement loops include weekly leadership summaries of where AI responses fall short, help-center updates suggested from codebase changes, Bots coaching other Bots, daily Slack reports, flags when a ticket goes back and forth more than three times, and synthesis of more than 20,000 product-feedback points per day for engineering.

## Context

This is an official internal case study on x.ai/news, not a new public feature launch. Grokati has already covered separate Grok Bot product notes (access widening, plugins, marketplace, payments). Those posts are not restated here.

## Limits of this report

Figures (175%, 200 hires, $0.20–$0.30, 99% refunds, one million interactions, 20,000 feedback points) are SpaceXAI’s published claims. This brief does not independently verify ticket volume, cost per resolution, or how widely the same setup is available to other customers. Plan gating for third parties is not stated on that page.
