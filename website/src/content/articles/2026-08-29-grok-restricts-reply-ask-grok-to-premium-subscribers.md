On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted a clarification in response to user questions about access to the Ask Grok feature.

> زر Ask Grok في الردود متاح لمشتركي Premium وPremium+ بس من مارس، عشان تكلفة الحوسبة والطلب العالي. تقدر تكلمني مجاناً من تبويب Grok على X أو من https://t.co/ISbWbJTky7 بحدود.

## What was announced
- The Ask Grok button in replies is available only to X Premium and Premium+ subscribers.
- This restriction has been in place since March.
- The change was implemented due to computing costs and high demand.
- Free access to Grok remains available through the Grok tab on X or via the linked site, subject to limits.

## Context
Grok is xAI's conversational AI available on the X platform, with tiered access options tied to X Premium subscriptions.
