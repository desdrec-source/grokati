On July 24, 2026, the official @grok account on X announced that Grok is now available in Google Workspace.

> Grok is now in Google Workspace.
> Install one add-on for Sheets, Slides, and Docs. Turn research into reports, build decks from outlines, and draft documents in place with Grok.

## What was announced
- Grok can be installed as one add-on that works across Google Sheets, Slides, and Docs.
- The integration supports turning research into reports, building decks from outlines, and drafting documents directly in the Workspace apps.

## Context
Grok is the AI developed by xAI. Google Workspace is the productivity suite that includes Docs, Sheets, and Slides.
