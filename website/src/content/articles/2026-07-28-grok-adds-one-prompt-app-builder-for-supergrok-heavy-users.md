On July 28, 2026, the official @grok account posted that app building is now available for SuperGrok Heavy users.

> Build apps from https://t.co/f3u3RwhHjk, iOS, and Android. With one prompt, turn an idea into a published product with its own domain.
> Now available for SuperGrok Heavy users.

## What was announced
- Users can build apps from the web, iOS, and Android.
- A single prompt converts an idea into a published product that includes its own domain.
- The capability is restricted to SuperGrok Heavy users.

## Context
Grok is the AI system developed by xAI and accessible via x.com and mobile apps.
