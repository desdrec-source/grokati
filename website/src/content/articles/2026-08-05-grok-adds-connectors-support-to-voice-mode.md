On August 5, 2026, the official @grok account on X stated that Connectors are now available in Voice Mode.

> Connectors are now available in Voice Mode.
> Ask Grok about your emails, check your daily meetings, or use any of your existing connectors, just by talking.

## What was announced
- Connectors can now be used within Voice Mode.
- Users may ask Grok about emails, daily meetings, or other existing connectors through spoken interaction.

## Context
Grok is the AI built by xAI. Voice Mode supports spoken conversations with Grok, and connectors provide integration with user data sources such as email and calendar services.
