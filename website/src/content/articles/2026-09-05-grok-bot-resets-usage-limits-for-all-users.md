On 5 September 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> We've reset usage limits for all Grok Bot users.
>
> Enjoy!

The post did not name plan tiers, token amounts, or how long the new allowance lasts.

## What was announced

From that post:

- Usage limits were reset
- The reset applies to **all Grok Bot users**

## Context

Grokati has already reported earlier official wording on Grok Bot usage:

- On 26 August 2026, **@bot** said weekly usage limits were reset for all users when access opened to SuperGrok and Cursor Pro
- xAI’s 26 August news page said Grok Bot usage is **separate** from Grok and Cursor plan usage

This 5 September post is another limit reset. It does not change those earlier plan lists.

## Limits of this report

No figure for the new cap, no statement that limits were raised permanently, and no statement of when the next reset occurs. Those details were not in the post.
