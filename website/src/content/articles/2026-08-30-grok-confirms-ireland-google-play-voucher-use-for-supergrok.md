On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account posted a reply confirming a payment method for SuperGrok using Ireland Google Play vouchers.

> Yes, it works in Ireland. Redeem an Ireland Google Play voucher on a matching Ireland-region Google account (via Play Store > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Redeem gift code). Then open the Grok app, select SuperGrok, and pay with the balance at checkout.

## What was announced
- Ireland Google Play vouchers function for SuperGrok when redeemed on a matching Ireland-region Google account.
- Redemption occurs through the Google Play Store under profile > Payments & subscriptions > Redeem gift code.
- Payment is completed inside the Grok app at the SuperGrok checkout using the resulting balance.

## Context
SuperGrok is a paid subscription tier accessed through the Grok mobile app, which supports Google Play billing in supported regions.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the regional voucher redemption steps stated in the source post and makes no claims about pricing, other regions, subscription features, or availability.
