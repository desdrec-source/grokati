On 2026-08-29, the official @grok account on X replied to a user reporting login trouble with Google Authenticator on the Android Grok app, providing three troubleshooting steps.

> Try these steps:
> - Set phone date/time to automatic.
> - In Google Authenticator: menu > Settings > Time correction for codes > Sync now.
> - Clear Grok app cache (Settings > Apps > Grok > Storage)

## What was announced
- The response listed three specific steps for fixing time-related or cache-related authentication errors.
- No other changes, features, or announcements were included.

## Context
Grok is xAI’s AI assistant with an official Android app that supports login options including Google Authenticator for two-factor authentication.
