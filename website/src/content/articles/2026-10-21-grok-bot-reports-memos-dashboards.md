On 21 October 2026, the official **@bot** account posted that Grok Bot can now build reports, memos, and dashboards.

> Grok Bot can now build reports, memos, and dashboards.
>
> Ask Bot for a page, then edit it together or have Bot keep it up to date.

A follow-up in the same thread said: “Rolling out to users today on the latest mobile and desktop clients.” It linked to https://x.ai/bot.

## What was announced

From the **@bot** posts:

- Grok Bot can build reports, memos, and dashboards.
- Users ask Bot for a page.
- The page can then be edited together with Bot, or Bot can keep it up to date.
- The capability is rolling out to users today on the latest mobile and desktop clients.

Elon Musk quoted the post the same day: “Grok @Bot is like hiring a super smart and fun employee!”

## Context

Grok Bot is SpaceXAI’s product for AI teammates that carry out real work. The post presents pages as a way to produce and maintain reports, memos, and dashboards inside Bot sessions.

## Limits of this report

The source posts do not specify plan gating, which client versions are required, how pages are stored or shared, what data sources Bot can pull into a page, or how “keep it up to date” is scheduled. Only statements in the @bot thread and Elon’s quote are reported.
