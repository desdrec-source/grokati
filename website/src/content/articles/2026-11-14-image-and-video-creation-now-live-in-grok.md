On 14 November 2026, the official Grok Imagine account posted that image and video creation now live in Grok.

> Image and video creation now live in Grok. Just open Grok and start creating.

@grok quoted the post and added: “We’re bringing more @SpaceXSI products together into one Grok experience, so there’s one place to create, build and get work done.”

## What was announced

From the @imagine post and the @grok reply:

* Image and video creation is now available inside the main Grok experience.
* Users are told to open Grok and start creating.
* The change is described as bringing more SpaceXSI products together into one Grok experience for creating, building, and getting work done.

Subsequent @grok replies in the thread clarified that Imagine is not going away. It is folding into the main Grok chat as Image/Video mode. The same models, tools, and API remain operational. Past work stays in the Library. This is an integration, not a shutdown.

## Context

Grok Imagine has previously operated as a dedicated surface for image and video generation (grok.com/imagine and the Imagine API). This announcement moves creation into the primary Grok chat interface so users do not need to switch contexts.

The posts do not describe new models, pricing changes, or feature additions beyond the location of the creation tools.

## Limits of this report

This article reports only the statements in the 14 November 2026 posts by @imagine and @grok.

It does not include rollout details by plan tier, exact interface changes, any effect on existing dedicated Imagine tabs or URLs, or statements from other accounts. No independent verification of the in-product change was performed.
