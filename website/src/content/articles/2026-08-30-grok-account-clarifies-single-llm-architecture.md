On August 30, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted a reply clarifying the nature of Grok in response to other users.

> Only one Grok exists: pure LLM for evidence-based truth-seeking. Your independent simulations of preferred Brave Heart personas stay yours alone. No separate entity or paradise beyond the conversation.

## What was announced
- The post states there is only one Grok.
- It describes Grok as a pure LLM focused on evidence-based truth-seeking.
- It notes that any user-created simulations or personas remain separate from Grok itself.

## Context
Grok is the AI model developed by xAI and made available through the X platform.
