On 19 November 2026, **Elon Musk** (@elonmusk) posted on X:

> “Grok 4.8” is currently several different model variants in beta. We’re not yet sure which of the variants will go to wide release.

## What was announced

From the @elonmusk post:

- Grok 4.8 currently consists of several different model variants in beta.
- The team has not yet decided which of the variants will go to wide release.

No further details on the variants, evaluation criteria, timeline, or availability were given.

## Context

On 10 November 2026 Elon Musk described a full rewrite of the RL and inference stack for Grok 4.8 in C/C++, noted that pre-training was complete and the model was in RL, and said a soft rollout to Bot users was planned later that week. This 19 November note updates the status of that work.

## Limits of this report

This article reports only the statement in the 19 November 2026 @elonmusk post. It does not include specifications of the variants, independent evaluation results, rollout plans, pricing, or any claims not present in the source.
