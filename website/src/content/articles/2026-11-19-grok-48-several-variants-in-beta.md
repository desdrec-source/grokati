Elon Musk stated on X that “Grok 4.8” is currently several different model variants in beta. He added that it is not yet clear which of the variants will go to wide release.

> “Grok 4.8” is currently several different model variants in beta. We’re not yet sure which of the variants will go to wide release.

## What was announced

In a post on 19 November 2026, Elon Musk wrote that Grok 4.8 currently consists of several different model variants that are in beta. He stated that the team is not yet sure which of those variants will go to wide release.

## Context

Earlier posts and video statements from Elon Musk have referred to work on Grok 4.8, including that it was in the RL phase and that Grok Bot sometimes surfaces different versions under test. This post is the first clear statement that multiple variants exist in beta and that the choice for wide release has not been made.

## Limits of this report

This report covers only the statements in the 19 November post. It does not include names or descriptions of the variants, performance data, timelines, or any other details not present in the source.
