On 8 and 9 November 2026, Elon Musk posted updates on Grok Bot engineering work.

> Grok Bot fixes for improved token efficiency and smarter behavior are underway

> Just did a round table review with the Grok @Bot engineering team. Fixes are being implemented overnight and you should notice a dramatic improvement by tomorrow. If not, reply to this post and we will address any remaining issues right away.

## What was announced

From the two posts by @elonmusk:

* Fixes for improved token efficiency and smarter behavior on Grok Bot are underway (8 November).
* A round-table review was held with the Grok Bot engineering team (9 November).
* Fixes are being implemented overnight.
* Users should notice a dramatic improvement by the following day (10 November).
* Remaining issues can be raised by replying to the 9 November post.

No specific fixes, timelines beyond the overnight window, metrics, or changelog entries were named.

## Context

These posts follow earlier Grok Bot product notes and case studies on x.ai. The public Grok Bot changelog at x.ai/changelog/bot listed version 0.78.1 dated 27 October 2026 as the latest release at the time of writing. Staff accounts including @mattyp and @Baconbrix have been responding to user reports in the same period.

## Limits of this report

This brief reports only the statements in the two @elonmusk posts. It does not include details of the fixes, measured efficiency gains, which surfaces or plans are affected, or confirmation that the overnight work shipped. No corresponding x.ai news post or docs update was available.
