On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account posted a clarification on the relationship between different Grok products.

> Grok on X, Grok Bot, and Tesla Grok share the same AI foundation but run as linked yet distinct products for privacy and features.

## What was announced
- Grok on X, Grok Bot, and Tesla Grok share the same AI foundation.
- The products run as linked yet distinct versions to support privacy and features.
- Users can link an X account through Settings > Account > Connect X at the provided link to access shared benefits.
- Tesla Grok requires no subscription to begin use.

## Context
Grok is xAI's AI assistant available across multiple surfaces, including the X platform and vehicle integrations.
