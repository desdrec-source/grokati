## What was announced
- Grok 4.5 is described as the most capable model yet.
- It is now accessible via the grok.com website, the X platform, and the official iOS and Android apps.

## Context
Grok is xAI's conversational AI model family, previously released in earlier versions on the same platforms mentioned in the post.
