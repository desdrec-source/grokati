On 8 September 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> You can now fill out forms and logins for your Bot without ever leaving the chat, with support for any password manager.

The official product video from that post is above. A follow-up linked to [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot).

## What was announced

From that **@bot** post:

- Forms and logins can be filled for a Bot **without leaving chat**
- Support for **any password manager**

## Context

This is a Grok Bot desktop/chat workflow note. It does not replace the earlier Microsoft or sales connector posts.

## Limits of this report

No list of supported password managers, platforms, or which form fields work is included. Those details were not in the post.
