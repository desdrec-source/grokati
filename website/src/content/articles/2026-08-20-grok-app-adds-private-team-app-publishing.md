On August 20, 2026, @Baconbrix announced a new capability in the Grok app for building and publishing private apps.

> New in the Grok app
> Build and publish private apps to share exclusively with members of your team.

## What was announced
- Users can now build and publish private apps within the Grok app.
- These apps are shared exclusively with members of a user's team.

## Context
The Grok app is the primary interface for interacting with xAI's Grok models and associated tools, including features such as Grok Build.
