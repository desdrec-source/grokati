On May 5, 2026, @LouiseGiam posted results from a survey testing identification of an xAI voice clone.

> Just over half of the people who responded (me included) thought the real human (voice B) was the AI. 🤯 Wow @xai
> 49% of survey participants correctly identified voice A as the clone.

## What was announced
- Just over half of respondents, including the poster, identified the real human voice (voice B) as AI.
- 49% of participants correctly identified voice A as the clone.

## Context
Grok includes a Voice feature that supports voice cloning.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the survey percentages and observations stated in the post and does not include methodology, sample size, or any official xAI statements.
