Grok Bot is now available on Android.

> Grok Bot is now available on Android.

## What was announced

On 2 September 2026 the official **@bot** account stated that Grok Bot is available on Android and linked the Google Play Store listing (package `ai.x.grok.bot`). A short product video accompanied the post. Staff accounts including @Baconbrix and @lingxi amplified the same announcement the same day.

## Context

Grok Bot is xAI’s always-on AI teammate product. Prior coverage on Grokati tracked plan expansions (SuperGrok / Cursor tiers) and plugin additions. This post adds native Android client availability.

## Limits of this report

This article reports only the statements and links in the official @bot posts. It does not include independent testing of feature parity with iOS/web, regional availability, plan gating, or performance on Android devices.
