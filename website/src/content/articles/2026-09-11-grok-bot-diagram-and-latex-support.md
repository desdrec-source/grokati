On 11 September 2026, SpaceXAI / Cursor staff member **@mattyp** posted that Grok Bot now supports diagram rendering and LaTeX in Bot responses.

> Grok Bot now supports diagram rendering and Latex in @Bot responses
>
> (You don't have to learn physics to take advantage of this feature)

The post included an image illustrating the capability.

## What was announced

From the **@mattyp** post:

- Diagram rendering is available inside Grok Bot responses
- LaTeX is supported in Grok Bot responses

No further technical details (syntax, supported diagram languages, or plan gating) were stated in the post.

## Context

@mattyp is a staff account associated with Cursor and Grok Bot at SpaceXAI. The announcement is a product capability update for the Bot surface rather than a model or platform release.

This report contains only the statement made in the source post. No independent test of rendering quality, supported diagram formats, or availability across plans is included.
