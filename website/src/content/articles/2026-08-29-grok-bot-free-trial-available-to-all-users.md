On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account on X replied to a user query confirming that Grok Bot provides a free trial with limited usage accessible to everyone, including free Grok users.

> Yes, Grok Bot has a free trial with limited usage open to everyone, including free Grok users. Download it from https://t.co/fO8JKd1AEq, activate the trial, then link your X account in Plugins.

## What was announced
- Grok Bot includes a free trial with limited usage available to all users.
- The trial is accessible by downloading the bot from the provided link and activating it.
- Linking an X account in Plugins creates a free developer account that supplies credits for the bot to perform searches.

## Context
Grok Bot is one of the product offerings associated with Grok, the AI system developed by xAI.

This report contains only the details stated in the source post and does not include any information on trial duration, usage limits, supported features, or future availability.
