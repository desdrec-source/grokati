On 22 September 2026, **@Tesla** posted:

> @Grok in your Tesla can now do meaningful work for you
>
> With Connectors, you can manage your inbox, clean up your calendar, or talk through existing files/chat/tasks – all hands-free

**@elonmusk** quoted that post: “Grok @Bot now in your Tesla!”

A follow-up from **@Tesla** in the same thread said Grok Bot can take on more complex tasks such as placing a usual coffee order, booking a reservation, or scheduling an appointment, and that this is **currently available to SuperGrok Heavy subscribers, with more tiers to follow**.

Another follow-up described how to add a Connector: open the Grok app, tap +, New Connector, pick the service and sign in; once connected, Grok can use it automatically.

## What was announced

From those official posts:

- Grok in Tesla can use Connectors for inbox, calendar, and existing files, chat, or tasks, hands-free
- Elon framed the change as Grok Bot in Tesla
- Example tasks named by Tesla: coffee order, reservation, appointment
- Availability stated as SuperGrok Heavy now, more tiers later
- Connectors are added in the Grok app via New Connector

## Context

Connectors in Voice Mode on phone and web were already an official @grok note earlier in 2026. This thread is specifically about the Tesla in-car surface and SuperGrok Heavy gating. It is not a model release.

## Limits of this report

The posts do not list which Connector services work in the vehicle, which Tesla software versions are required, or a date for other subscription tiers. Those details were not stated.
