Grok Bot can now fill out forms and logins without leaving the chat.

> You can now fill out forms and logins for your Bot without ever leaving the chat, with support for any password manager.

## What was announced

On 8 September 2026 the official **@bot** account stated that Bots can fill forms and logins while remaining inside the conversation, and that the feature works with any password manager. A short product video was included. A follow-up linked to https://x.ai/bot.

The same day, staff member **@mattyp** listed the change among current Grok Bot updates (alongside iPad/Android availability, Enterprise, the templates marketplace, and performance improvements) and posted a longer video.

## Context

Grok Bot is the always-on AI teammate product that runs on its own computer, signs into tools, and returns finished work. Prior Grokati coverage tracked plan expansions, Microsoft plugins, the public templates marketplace, and Android availability. Password-manager support reduces friction when a Bot needs to authenticate inside web apps or forms.

## Limits of this report

This article reports only the statements in the official @bot post and the same-day staff summary. It does not include independent testing of which password managers work, how credentials are handled, plan gating, or security details. Those points were not stated in the source posts.
