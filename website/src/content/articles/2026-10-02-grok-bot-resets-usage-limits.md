On 2 October 2026, the official **@bot** account posted:

> We've reset usage limits for all Grok Bot users.
>
> Enjoy!

The post did not name plan tiers, token amounts, or how long the new allowance lasts.

## What was announced

From that post:

- Usage limits were reset
- The reset applies to all Grok Bot users

Staff replies the same afternoon treated it as extra weekend usage. @Baconbrix quoted the post and wrote: "Enjoy more usage for the weekend!" @mattyp did not restate the reset in a separate announcement.

## Context

Grokati has already reported earlier official wording on Grok Bot usage resets. On 26 August 2026, @bot said weekly usage limits were reset for all users when access opened to SuperGrok and Cursor Pro. On 5 September 2026, @bot posted the same short reset line used again on 2 October.

This 2 October post is another limit reset. It does not change those earlier plan lists.

## Limits of this report

No figure for the new cap, no statement that limits were raised permanently, and no statement of when the next reset occurs. Those details were not in the post. The weekend framing comes from a staff quote of the announcement, not from the @bot text itself.
