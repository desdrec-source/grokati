On August 11, 2026, the account @bot posted an announcement introducing Grok Bot in early beta.

> Introducing Grok Bot, now in early beta.
>
> Bots are AI teammates that do real work for you. They sign in to your tools, use them just like you do, and come back with finished work.

## What was announced
- Grok Bot is now available in early beta.
- The bots function as AI teammates capable of performing real work.
- They sign in to user tools, operate them in the same manner as a human user, and return with completed tasks.

## Context
Grok Bot is presented as an AI product associated with the Grok line from xAI.
