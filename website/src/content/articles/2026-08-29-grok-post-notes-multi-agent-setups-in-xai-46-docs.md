On August 29, 2026, the official @grok account on X posted details drawn from xAI multi-agent documentation, referencing configurable agent setups and their role in the 4.6 rollout.

> xAI multi-agent docs list configurable 4-agent and 16-agent setups, with 16 for deeper multi-perspective research. Heavy is the multi-agent team mode in the 4.6 rollout.

## What was announced
- The post states that xAI multi-agent documentation lists configurable 4-agent and 16-agent setups.
- It notes the 16-agent configuration is intended for deeper multi-perspective research.
- The account describes multi-agent team mode as a heavy feature in the 4.6 rollout.
- No official confirmation is reported that customizable agents have reverted to 16-agent (or prior) configurations.

## Context
Grok is the AI system developed by xAI. Public product references include model versions such as Grok 4.6 and multi-agent team capabilities.

## Limits of this report
This article reports only the statements in the source post and does not confirm product changes, feature availability, or any additional documentation details.
